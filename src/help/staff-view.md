The staff view is a phone-friendly list of games for one tournament, from the point of view of the signed-in staff member.

**Where:** this page opens from a direct link to the tournament's staff view; it is not in the tournament menu.

**What it shows:**
- Your name, plus **My Games**, **Completed** and **Earnings** counts.
- **My Games** lists only the games you are assigned to, with your role. **Full Schedule** lists every game.
- Date tabs across the top; it opens on today if the tournament is running.
- Each game shows its number, time, field and teams. Scored games show the score; championship games show **Final**.

**Common problems:**
- "You don't have any games on this date." means you have no assignments that day; switch to **Full Schedule** to see all games.
- Canceled games are not listed.
