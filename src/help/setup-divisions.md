The **Divisions** section of Tournament setup decides which divisions clubs can pick when they register, in what order, and what status badge they see.

**Where:** **Setup → Tournament setup → Divisions**.

**To pick divisions:**
1. Tick the divisions this tournament offers. Click a ticked division name to rename it.
2. To add your own, type it in the box under **Custom divisions** (for example "Boys U9 (7v7)") and click **Add**.
3. Under **Division order**, drag (or use the arrows) to set the order teams see on the registration form and public pages.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

**Save as my defaults** remembers this division list in this browser; **Load my defaults** brings it back in another tournament. **Clear all** unticks everything.

**To show a registration status badge:**
1. Under **Registration status**, pick **Filling up**, **Closing soon** or **Full** (or **No badge**), and optionally a closing date.
2. Type your own wording in the text box, or leave it blank to use the wording shown. The **Preview** shows the badge.
3. For each division, pick a **Status** (**Open**, **Limited**, **Almost full**, **Few left**, **Last spots**, **Filling fast** or **Full**). For the amber ones you can type a number of **Spots** to show instead.

The badge shows on the event page, the org home page and the tournaments list.

**Optional extras:** **Age & eligibility chart link** and a **Divisions note** both show under Divisions on the public event page.

**Common problems:**
- Marking a division **Full** does not close it. Teams can still register; sort it out with them, or turn team registration off under **Registration types**.
- Teams, pools and games are managed on **Setup → Divisions & teams**, not here.
