Every staff member has a staff ID badge with their photo and role. Print it for a lanyard or add it to Apple Wallet on your phone.

**Where:** on your home page, tap **My staff ID** under **My events**.

**To print it:**
1. Tap **Print my ID**.
2. It prints at true badge size (2.125 by 3.375 inches). Cut along the card edge and punch the marked hole for a lanyard.

**To add it to your phone:** tap **Add to Apple Wallet**. This button only appears when your organization has Wallet passes turned on.

Tap **My portal** to go back to your home page.

**Common problems:**
- **Your login isn't linked to a staff record yet.** Contact your coordinator.
