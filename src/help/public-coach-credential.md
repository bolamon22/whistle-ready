After you submit the coach waiver you get a coach credential: a card with your name, teams and a QR code for check-in.

**Where:** the link in the email you get after submitting the coach waiver.

1. Open the link. The page shows **Coach credential** with your name and teams.
2. Tap **Save to phone** to download it. On iPhone you can also press and hold the card to add it to Photos.
3. Tap **Print card** to print it at badge size for a standard lanyard holder.
4. Below the card you can see the **Event**, **Dates**, **Club**, **Credential ID** and the date your **Waiver signed**.
5. Tap **Add another team** to open the coach waiver form again.

**Common problems:**
- You do not need to bring the credential. Your waiver on file is what matters; the card just speeds things up at the coaches' tent.
- Lost the email? Check your spam folder, or ask the organizer to look you up.
