Follow a team on the schedule page to keep it under **My Teams** and get phone alerts about it. No account needed; follows are saved on the phone or browser you use.

**Where:** the **Follow** button next to each team on the schedule page (/tournaments/<id>/public): in pool lists, standings, search results and schedule games.

1. Find your team and tap **Follow**. It changes to **Following**. Tap it again to unfollow.
2. The first time you follow on a phone, a box asks **Get alerts for** your team. Tap **Turn on alerts** and allow notifications, or **Not now**.
3. Your followed teams show under **My Teams** at the top of the page, with each team's next game.
4. If alerts are off, tap **Turn on alerts** in **My Teams** any time. **Alerts on** means this phone will get them.

Alerts cover: when the schedule is out, when and where your team plays, if a game moves, and every final score. Nothing else.

**Common problems:**
- iPhone: alerts only work after you add the schedule to your Home Screen. In Safari tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then open it from there and turn on alerts.
- "Notifications are blocked for this site" means your browser is blocking them. Allow notifications in your browser settings, then follow again.
- Follows belong to one phone. Follow again on each phone that should get alerts.
- A club can have teams with the same name in different divisions. Follow the one in your division.
- You can follow up to 40 teams per event.
- Follower counts under team names show how many people follow that team.
