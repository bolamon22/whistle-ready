The **Work at our event** form is where referees, scorekeepers, trainers and event staff apply to work your events.

**Where:** Home → **Admin** → **Forms** → **Work at our event** (admins). Directors can open [Forms](/dashboard/org/forms) directly.

**To edit the staff application form:**
1. Open **Work at our event** and click **Edit**. The public link is shown at the top with **View** and **Copy**.
2. Tick **Show a "Work With Us" link on your public site** to list it on your site.
3. Add a **Banner image** (**Upload image** or **Remove**). It shows behind the page title.
4. Edit the **Intro text**.
5. List **Positions (comma separated)**, for example Referee / Official, Scorekeeper.
6. List **Officiating certification levels (comma separated)**. These appear, with a Boys / Girls / Both question, when an applicant picks a referee or official role.
7. Set the **Age confirmation (leave blank to hide)** line.
8. Set the **Confirmation title** and **Confirmation message**, and tick **Email a confirmation to the applicant** if wanted.
9. In **Notify me at**, enter who should be emailed when someone applies. Use **Add person** to notify someone only for certain events.
10. Click **Save**.

The section footer shows how many applications you have.

**Common problems:**
- Unticking the "Work With Us" link hides it from your site menu, but the form still works by direct link.
- **Notify me at** left blank uses your org contact address.
