Score tables shows, for each game, the scorekeeper on it and whether they've checked in with the **Check in** button on their staff app. It's for field directors, assigners, schedulers and directors.

**Where:** **Score tables** on the field director home page, or **Live → Score tables** on the event dashboard.

**To read it:**
1. Pick the day at the top. Today opens first on game day.
2. The tiles count who's checked in, games starting without their scorekeeper, and games with no scorekeeper.
3. Each time is a list of fields. **In 9:42** means they checked in. **Not in yet** turns amber 30 minutes before the game and red 10 minutes before. **No scorekeeper** means nobody is assigned.
4. Tap the phone or message button to call or text that scorekeeper.
5. Tick **Only tables that need someone** to hide the covered ones.

The page refreshes every minute. A scorekeeper who works a field all day checks in for each game, so an early game checked in doesn't cover the later ones.

**Common problems:**
- A scorekeeper is at the table but shows **Not in yet**: they haven't tapped **Check in**. Ask them to tap it on their staff home.
- No game shows: games need a day, a time and a field on the schedule first.
