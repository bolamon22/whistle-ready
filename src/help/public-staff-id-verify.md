Every staff ID card has a QR code. Scanning it opens a page that confirms whether the person is current event staff.

**Where:** scan the QR code on the staff member's ID card with your phone's camera.

1. Point your camera at the QR code on the card and tap the link that appears.
2. Wait a moment while it shows "Checking credential…".
3. A valid card shows the person's name, their roles (for example **Referee**, **Scorekeeper**, **Field Ops**, **Athletic Trainer** or **Assigner**) and **Verified event staff** with the organizer's name.
4. Check that the name matches the person in front of you.

**Common problems:**
- **Credential not found** means the ID doesn't match an active staff record. Check with the event director.
