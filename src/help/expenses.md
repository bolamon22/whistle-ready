When someone buys something for the event (water, ice, paint for the goals), they turn in the receipt from their phone. You approve it and mark when you paid them back, on **Financials → Expenses**.

**Where:** **Financials** in the tournament menu, then the **Expenses** tab. The orange number on the tab is how many are waiting for approval.

**What is on it:**
- Tiles: **Spent (approved)**, **To pay back**, **Waiting for approval** and **Paid back**, with what was spent by category under them.
- **Waiting for approval**: each new receipt with its photo, who bought it, the store, the day and how they paid. Tap **Approve**, or **Open** to see it bigger.
- **Who to pay back**: one line per person, with everything approved that they paid for with their own money, their payout details from the Staff Pool (Zelle, Venmo, check) and the total.
- **All receipts**: every receipt and where it stands. The buttons above it show **All**, **Waiting**, **To pay back**, **Paid back**, **Company paid** or **Not approved**.

**To pay someone back:**
1. Pay them the usual way (Zelle, Venmo, a check).
2. Under **Who to pay back**, tap **Mark paid back** on their line.
3. Pick how you paid (**Paid by**), the day (**On**) and a **Note** if you like (a check number), then tap **Mark $… paid back**. That covers all of their approved receipts at once.

To mark just one receipt, open it and tap **Mark paid back**. Made a mistake? Open it and tap **Undo paid back**.

**To turn one down:** open it, tap **Not approved…**, say why (they see your reason) and tap **Not approved**. **Back to waiting** undoes it.

**To add one yourself** (for someone without a login, or a receipt handed to you):
1. Tap **Add an expense**.
2. Tap **Snap the receipt** or **Photo or PDF**. It reads the store, the date and the total and fills them in; check each one.
3. Fill in **Who bought it** (pick a name from the Staff roster to see how they get paid, or type any name), **What was bought**, **From (store)**, **Total**, **Date bought** and **Category**, and pick **Paid with**: **Their own money**, **Company card** or **Company cash**.
4. Leave **Approved** ticked, tick **Already paid back** if you have, and tap **Add expense**.

**To change or delete one:** open it and tap the pencil. Change anything in the form, or tap the trash can, then **Delete**. A receipt marked paid back can't be deleted or turned down: undo the paid back first.

**Download** saves a spreadsheet (CSV) of every receipt for the event, for your books.

**Good to know:**
- Approved receipts count as a cost on **Overview**, as **Receipts & purchases**. Company card and company cash count as paid right away; their own money counts as owed until you mark it paid back. Don't also add receipts on **Other entries**.
- **Possible duplicate**: another receipt at this event has the same amount, day and store (one turned in by staff and one you typed, say). **Receipt says $…**: the total on the photo isn't the amount typed. **No receipt**: nobody added a photo.
- Receipt photos are private. Only the person who turned it in, the tournament director and admins can open them.
