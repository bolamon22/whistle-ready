Organizer answers about divisions and team placement.

**Can we move to a lower division?**
Yes. As long as you ask early and there's open space in the lower division, we're happy to move your team. Once the schedule is published, a move can only happen if another team is willing to swap places with you. To keep play fair and competitive, register in the division that fits your team's age and skill level. To request a change, email info@sunshinelax.com as soon as possible.

**How many teams make a division?**
A division needs at least 4 teams. An A/B split needs at least 4 teams willing to play A.

**What if our division doesn't fill?**
If a division doesn't reach 4 teams, we'll tell you well ahead and work out options with you.

**Will our two teams in the same division play each other?**
We do everything possible to keep teams from the same club apart in pool play. The rare exception is a very small division where it can't be avoided. In playoffs and brackets, matchups come from seeding, so teams from the same club may meet based on how they play.

**Which teams are in my division?**
Once teams are placed, they're listed on the event's schedule page.
