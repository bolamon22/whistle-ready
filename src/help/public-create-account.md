An account lets parents keep track of tournaments and teams. Club directors and staff usually don't need this page: club directors choose a password when they register their teams, and staff get a setup link by email.

**Where:** on the sign-in page, tap **Create one** next to "Don't have an account?".

1. Fill in **Full Name** and **Email**.
2. Choose a **Password** (at least 6 characters) and type it again in **Confirm Password**.
3. Tap **Create Account**. You're signed in straight away.

Already have an account? Tap **Sign in** at the bottom instead.

**Common problems:**
- "An account with this email already exists": use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page instead of creating a new account.
- "Passwords do not match." Retype both password boxes.
- Registered a team? Don't create a separate account here. Sign in with your contact email and the password you chose on the registration form. Didn't choose one? Use the team account link in your registration email (see "Set up your team account from the email link").
- Joining as staff? Use the signup link from your assigner (see "Join the staff from a signup or invite link").
