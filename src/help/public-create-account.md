An account lets parents keep track of their players and teams, and coaches follow their team's games. Club directors and staff get their accounts another way, and this page points them there.

**Where:** tap **Create account** at the top of an event page, or **Create one** on the sign-in page.

1. Under **Who is it for?**, tap **Referee, scorekeeper, trainer or event staff**, **Parent or player**, or **Club director or coach**.
2. **Parent or player:** fill in **Full Name** and **Email**, choose a **Password** (at least 6 characters), type it again in **Confirm Password**, and tap **Create Account**. You're signed in straight away.
3. **Referee, scorekeeper, trainer or event staff:** staff accounts come from the event organizer. Open the sign-up link your assigner sent you, or tap **Work with** and your organizer's name to apply. An account made on this page is a parent account and can't be put on games.
4. **Club director or coach:** club directors tap **Email me my portal link** (your club portal comes with your team registration). Registering a team needs no account first: tap **Find your event**. Coaches who want to follow their team's games tap **Create a coach account**.

Already have an account? Tap **Sign in** at the bottom instead.

**Common problems:**
- "An account with this email already exists": use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page instead of creating a new account.
- "Passwords do not match." Retype both password boxes.
- Registered a team? Don't create a separate account here. Sign in with your contact email and the password you chose on the registration form. Didn't choose one? Use the team account link in your registration email (see "Set up your team account from the email link").
- Joining as staff? Use the signup link from your assigner (see "Join the staff from a signup or invite link"). Made a parent account by mistake? Use the signup link with the same email and password, and staff is added to that account.
