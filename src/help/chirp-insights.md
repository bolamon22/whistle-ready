**Chirp insights** shows the questions your staff and attendees ask Chirp, so you can spot gaps and turn common questions into a FAQ.

**Where:** **Live → Chirp insights**.

**What is on it:**
- **Staff questions**: questions asked in the floating Chirp or **Help → Ask Chirp**, with who asked and from which page. Questions Chirp could not answer from the manual are tagged **Not in manual**.
- **Public questions**: **Most asked** and **Recent questions** from visitors.

**To see only the misses:** click **Couldn't answer** next to **All** under **Staff questions**.

**To turn questions into a FAQ:**
1. Under **Turn questions into a FAQ**, click **Generate FAQ suggestions**.
2. Review each drafted answer.
3. Click **Add to event page** on the good ones. They are added to the event page (edit them later in **Setup → Tournament setup → Page builder**).

**Common problems:**
- "No clear themes yet" means there are not enough questions yet; check back later.
