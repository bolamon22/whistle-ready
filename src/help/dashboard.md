The **Dashboard** is the tournament at a glance: teams registered, games, staff coverage and money.

**Where:** **Dashboard** in the tournament menu.

**What is on it:**
- **At a glance** tiles: **Teams**, **Games**, **Staff assigned** and **Collected**. Click a tile to jump to that page.
- **Live**: shortcuts to **Post scores**, **Assignments** and **Communications**.
- **Registered teams**: **Teams by division**. Tap a division to see its teams. Teams on the waiting list show a **Waiting list** tag.
- **Money**: **Revenue**, **Staff expenses**, **Gross profit**, **Balance due** and **Collection progress**, with a link to **View full financials**.

**To show or hide money:**
1. Click the **Money hidden** pill next to **At a glance** (or **Show** next to **Money**).
2. Amounts, the **Collected** tile and the **Paid**, **Partial** and **Unpaid** marks on teams appear. Click **Money shown** to hide them again.

Money starts hidden every time you open the dashboard, so it is safe to turn the screen toward someone.

**To open or close registration:** click the **Registration open** / **Registration closed** pill. It takes you to **Setup → Tournament setup → Registration types**.

**To reorder the division tiles:** drag the grip on the left of a division. The order is saved for the tournament.

**To copy this event for next year:** use **Copy tournament** under **Start next year's event from this one**.

**Common problems:**
- With no registrations yet, the page shows **Share registration form** instead of team lists.
