**Staff pay** totals what each staff member earned at this tournament and lets you mark them paid. **Time entries** tracks hours for hourly staff.

**Where:** **People → Staff pay**, or the **Staff pay** and **Time entries** tabs on **People → Staff roster**. **Staff pay** is also a button on the tournament card and on **Financials**.

**On the Whistle Ready Assigner plan:** above the list, **Tournament director** names the event's tournament director, with **Edit** (or **Add them**) to open their page and **See their view**, and **Assigning fee** sets what they pay you on top (see "Share an event with your tournament director"). **Officials are paid by** says who pays the officials at this event (the tournament director, or **You**), and **Mark Paid** has **Paid by** for one payment; each paid person shows who paid them. **Record payment** logs what the tournament director has paid you: your fee plus any staff pay you paid for them. People the tournament director marks paid show paid here too.

**To change one game's pay:** open the person (the arrow on the right), click the game's amount, enter the pay for that game and, if you like, why (for example "covered two halves"), then **Save**. The game shows **edited**; hover over it to see who set it, the note and what it was. Pay you set by hand stays when rates or someone's override change. **Use the rate** puts it back to what the pay rates give. Someone already marked paid can't be changed here; click **Unpay** first. Assigners can change pay only for people whose pay is all in their groups.

**To pay staff:**
1. Open **Staff pay**. The header shows **Total**, **Paid** and **Outstanding**.
2. Search by name or filter by **Role:**. **Clear filters** resets.
3. Each person lists their games (or hours) and a **Total Payout**.
4. Click **Mark Paid**, add notes if you like, then **✓ Confirm**.
5. To undo, click **Unpay** (you are asked to confirm).

**To export:** click **CSV** for a spreadsheet of every assignment and its pay.

**Pay rates** (directors and Head Assigners) opens this event's rates and officials per game. See "Set staff pay rates and officials per game".

**Assigners see only their own groups.** Staff pay is split into **Boys refs**, **Girls refs**, **Scorekeepers** and **Field ops & trainers**. An assigner sees just the groups picked for them, and the page says which, for example "Showing pay for **Boys refs**". A ref slot goes by the game's division; a division that says neither boys nor girls goes by the ref's own **Can ref** setting.
- Someone who also has pay in a group the assigner doesn't run shows **Your part only**, with **Director marks paid** instead of **Mark Paid**. One payment covers all of a person's pay at the event, so the director pays them.
- The same groups decide which pay an assigner sees on **Assignments**, the staff roster and the Staff Pool.
- Admins pick the groups on **Logins**, directors on **Your team**. Until then an assigner sees boys and girls refs, not scorekeepers or hourly staff.

**To track hourly time (Time entries):**
1. Pick the day tab.
2. Under **Quick Punch Clock**, click **Clock In** for a staff member and **Clock Out** when they finish.
3. Forgot to clock? Click **+ Manual Entry**, choose the staff member, and enter **Clock In**, **Clock Out** or **Manual Hours**, plus **Notes**. Click **Save**.
4. Fix a row with **Edit**, or remove it with **Del**.

Running entries show **● LIVE**; hand-edited ones show **edited**.

**Common problems:**
- Only hourly staff appear on the punch clock: people in the Staff Pool whose role is **Athletic Trainer** or **Field Operations**. "No hourly staff on roster." means there are none yet.
- Other paid staff (a gate or parking worker, say): add them to the Staff Pool as **Field Operations** with an **Hourly Rate**, then log their hours here.
- Per-game rates come from **Pay rates** (also **Setup → Tournament setup → Staff pay rates**). Time entries are paid at the **Hourly Rate** on each person's Staff Pool card.
