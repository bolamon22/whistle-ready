**1099 export** lists what each person was paid in a calendar year, so you (or your accountant) can prepare their 1099-NEC forms.

**Where:** **Staff → 1099 export** in the top bar. Directors (the Head Assigner on the Assigner plan) and admins only.

**To get the list:**
1. Pick the **year**. In January it starts on last year.
2. Tick **Only people who need a 1099** to hide everyone under the IRS line ($600 for 2025 and before, $2,000 for payments made in 2026).
3. Click **Download CSV** for a spreadsheet: payer, name, email, phone, total paid, whether they need a 1099, whether a W-9 is on file, events, payments and pay method.

**What counts:** every payment marked on **Staff pay** with **Mark paid** (yours, and your tournament directors' in their area), in the year of the date it was marked paid. Pay that was earned but not yet marked paid isn't in it.

**Who pays them:** whoever pays the staff files their 1099s. Each payment is listed under whoever paid it: on the Assigner plan that's the event's **Officials are paid by** on **Staff pay** (the tournament director unless you change it), or the **Paid by** picked when someone was marked paid. A tournament director with no name yet shows as "Tournament director of" the event. With more than one payer, pick one from **Every payer** to see and download just their list. The IRS line is per payer.

**Address and tax ID:** they aren't in the export. They're on each person's W-9 (open it from their Staff Pool profile). **Of them, no W-9** counts the people who need a 1099 but haven't uploaded one.

**Common problems:**
- Someone is missing: they were never marked paid that year on any event's **Staff pay**.
- The same person shows twice under one payer: they have two Staff Pool records. Merge them on the Staff Pool (see "Merge duplicate staff records").
- This list is a starting point for your filing, not tax advice; check with your accountant.
