Every coach, assistant coach and team manager on the sideline completes the coach waiver once per event. If you coach more than one team, pick them all and you get one credential that covers them.

**Where:** **Coach waiver** in the event page's **Event info** menu, or **Coach waiver & credential** under **For coaches** on the event page.

1. Under **Coach & Contact**, fill in **Coach Full Name**, **Email Address** and **Mobile Phone** (ten digits).
2. Under **Teams You Are Coaching**, tick every team you will be on the sideline for.
3. Fill in **Club / Organization** and pick a **Coaching Role**: **Head Coach**, **Assistant Coach** or **Team Manager**. **Team Division / Age Group** fills in from the teams you picked.
4. Fill in **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Phone**. Some events also ask whether your team is staying at a hotel or vacation rental.
5. Optional: **Add a photo** for your credential, and fill in **Experience & Certifications**.
6. Under **Waiver & Agreements**, read each section and tick every box. Then type your full legal name as your signature. It must match the name you entered at the top.
7. Tap **Submit & get my credential**.

When it goes through you will see a confirmation (usually **You are credentialed**). Your credential is emailed to the address you entered.

**Common problems:**
- The submit button stays off until everything is done. The **Still needed** line tells you what is missing.
- Your credential stays pending until every agreement is ticked and signed.
- If your team isn't listed, it may not be registered yet. Contact the organizer.
- **Coach registration is closed for this event** means the organizer has turned this form off.
