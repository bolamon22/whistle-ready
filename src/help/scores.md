Post scores lists every game so you can type in final scores quickly, check what's missing, and fix mistakes. Scores feed the standings and brackets on the public page.

**Where:** **Live → Post scores**, or **Post scores** on your home page's game day tools.

**To find a game:**
- Type in **Search teams or field…**.
- Choose **Show by division** or **Show by complex / field**, then pick a division or field from the list (on a computer, from the left side).
- Sort with **Sort by date/time** or **Sort by game #**.
- Filter with **Show all games**, **Show final games** or **Show not final games**.

**To post a final score:**
1. Type each team's score in the boxes next to the team names.
2. Press Enter or Tab to save and jump to the next box. Scores also save when you leave the box.
3. Once both scores are in, the **Final** box is ticked.

**To fix a score:** type the correct number over the old one and move out of the box. To clear a score, delete both numbers.

The count at the top right shows how many games are scored out of the total.

**Common problems:**
- During the event's dates, saving a final alerts phones following either team. A corrected score replaces the earlier alert on their phones.
- **Enter both scores first.** You can't tick **Final** with one box empty.
- The **Note…** box is not saved.
