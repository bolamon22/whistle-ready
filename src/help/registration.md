The registrations page has its own **Settings** so you can adjust pricing and the division list without leaving it. It edits the same pricing as **Setup → Tournament setup → Team fees** (see "Set team fees and the confirmation letter").

**Where:** People → Team registrations → **Settings**

**To change pricing or divisions:**
1. Open **People → Team registrations** and click **Settings**.
2. Edit the pricing, then under **Divisions** add, remove or reorder divisions.
3. Click **Save Settings**.

**Common problems:**
- Changing pricing does not rewrite invoices that already exist. Open a registration with **Edit** to re-check its **Invoice Amount**.
