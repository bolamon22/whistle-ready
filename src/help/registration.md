Team fees decide what each club is invoiced when it registers. The same pricing drives the live fee estimate on your public registration form and the invoice on every registration.

**Where:** Setup → Tournament setup → **Team fees** (or **People → Team registrations → Settings**)

**To set the per-team rate:**
1. Under **Per-team rate by number of teams**, enter the price for each tier. The last tier is open-ended (for example "7+ teams").
2. Click **Add tier** to add a step, or the × button to remove one. You always keep at least one tier.
3. Click **Save Changes** at the top of Tournament setup.

**To charge a different rate for some divisions:**
1. Click **Add division rate**.
2. Give it a name (for example "Youth") and type the text after **divisions containing** (for example "7v7"). Every division whose name contains that text uses this rate instead of the per-team rate.

**To add an early-bird discount:**
1. Click **Add early-bird discount**.
2. Enter a label and an amount, pick **$ off** or **% off**, and choose the **if registered by** date. The discount is per team.

**To edit pricing and the division list from the registrations page:**
1. Open **People → Team registrations** and click **Settings**.
2. Edit the pricing, then under **Divisions** add, remove or reorder divisions.
3. Click **Save Settings**.

**Common problems:**
- **Reset to defaults** (Team fees) replaces your tiers with the standard pricing.
- Changing pricing does not rewrite invoices that already exist. Open a registration with **Edit** to re-check its **Invoice Amount**.
- The **Confirmation letter** below the fees is what clubs see and are emailed after registering. See [Who gets new-registration emails](/dashboard/org/forms) for the internal heads-up.
