Photo bookings is your record of families who booked a photographer for this tournament. Families book photographers directly; you don't approve anything here.

**Where:** People → Photo bookings

**What you see:** each booking's **Photographer**, who it was **Booked by**, the **Player** (with club and division), the **Package** and **When**.

**To see one photographer's bookings:** click their name in the row of buttons at the top. Click **All** to go back.

**To open a booking:** click the row to see every detail. From there:
- **Their page** opens the photographer's public page.
- **Email the family** opens an email to the family.
- **Delete** removes the booking for good (you'll be asked to confirm).

**To download the list:** click **Export CSV**.

If there are no bookings yet, **Manage photographers** takes you to your photographers list.
