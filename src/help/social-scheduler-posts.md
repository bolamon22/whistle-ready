The **Social scheduler** schedules Instagram and Facebook posts. Approve a post once and it publishes itself at the scheduled time. Nothing publishes without approval.

**Where:** top bar → **Social**, or Home → **Admin** → **Social scheduler**.

**To create a post:**
1. Click **Create post**, or the + on any day in the **Calendar**.
2. Under **Post to**, pick the accounts. You get one post per account, and approving one approves the set.
3. Add a photo or video (up to 10 for a carousel).
4. Under **Post as**, choose the feed, a **Story**, or both.
5. Write the **Caption**, set the **Publish date**, and add a **First comment** if wanted.
6. Click **Save as draft**, **Save & approve**, or **Add to queue** for the next open queue time.

**To approve posts:** open **Approvals**. Posts are grouped as **Needs approval**, **Scheduled**, **Published** and **Failed**. Click **Approve** on a draft.

**Other actions on a post:** **Publish now** (then **Yes, publish**), **Unapprove** to move it back to needs approval, **Retry** on a failed post, and **Save changes**.

**Ideas:** turn on ideas to see suggested posts on the calendar. Click **Ask Chirp** to brainstorm. Ideas are suggestions only.

**Common problems:**
- Only directors and admins can approve. Others can save drafts, marked "Waiting on a director".
- **Create post** is grayed out until an account is connected.
- You cannot pick a date that has passed, and published posts cannot be moved.
- Publishing checks every 15 minutes, so a post can go out a few minutes after its time.
