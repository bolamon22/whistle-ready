Staff applications lists everyone who applied to work this tournament through your work form.

**Where:** People → Staff applications

**To review applications:**
1. Each row shows the applicant's **Name**, **Contact**, **Positions** and when they **Submitted**. The newest is at the top.
2. Click a row to see every answer they gave.

**To share the application form:** click **Open form** and copy the link.

**To download the list:** click **Export CSV**.

**Common problems:**
- This page is a read-only list. Applications can't be edited or deleted here.
