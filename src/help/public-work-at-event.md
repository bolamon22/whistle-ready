Officials, scorekeepers, athletic trainers and event staff can apply to work an event. The organizer reviews applications and contacts you about open positions.

**Where:** **Work at our event** in the event page's **Event info** menu, or /tournaments/<id>/work.

1. Under **About you**, fill in **Full name**, **Email** and **Mobile phone**.
2. If shown, tick the events you want to work under **Which events?**
3. Under **Positions**, tick every role you are interested in (for example **Referee / Official**, **Scorekeeper**, **Field / Event staff**, **Athletic trainer / Medical**).
4. If you pick an officiating role, choose your **Certification level** and **I officiate** (**Boys**, **Girls** or **Both**).
5. Under **Experience & availability**, add your **Relevant experience**, **Certifications / credentials** and the days you can work.
6. Add anything else in **Anything else?**
7. Confirm the age requirement if shown, then tap **Submit application**.

You will see a confirmation (usually **Application received!**) and a summary under **Your application**.

**Common problems:**
- Pick at least one position (and at least one event, when events are listed) or the form will not submit.
- The position list can differ by event.
- After you apply, the organizer will reach out about open positions.
