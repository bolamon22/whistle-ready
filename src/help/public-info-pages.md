Our website has information pages written by the organizer, such as rules, refunds, weather, FAQs and guides for families. Which pages exist, and what they're called, is up to the organizer.

**Where:** the website menu at the top (some pages are grouped in a drop-down, and **More** holds extra pages), and the link lists at the very bottom of every page.

1. Open the menu. On a phone, tap the menu icon at the top right.
2. Tap a page name, or tap a group name to open its drop-down and pick a page.
3. If you don't see what you need, scroll to the bottom of the page. Guides and other pages may be listed there under their own headings.
4. Contact details (email, phone, hours) and social media links are also at the bottom of every page.
5. Tap **Back** at the top of an info page to return to the home page.

**Common problems:**
- "This page has no content yet." means the organizer hasn't filled that page in. Use the email or phone at the bottom of the page instead.
