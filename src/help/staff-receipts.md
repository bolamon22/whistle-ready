Bought something for an event? Turn in the receipt from your home page and the office pays you back.

**Where:** the **Receipts** card on your home page. It shows when there's an event you can turn a receipt in for.

**To turn in a receipt:**
1. Tap **Turn in a receipt**.
2. Tap **Snap the receipt** and take a photo of it, or tap **Photo or PDF** to pick one you already have. It reads the store, the date and the total and fills them in. Check each one.
3. Pick the **Event**, and fill in **What did you buy?**, **From (store)**, **Total**, **Date bought** and **Category** where they're empty.
4. Under **How did you pay?** pick **My own money (pay me back)**, **Company card** or **Company cash**.
5. Add **Notes** if the office needs to know something, and tap **Turn it in**.

No receipt? You can still turn it in: say why in the notes.

**To see where it stands:** your receipts are listed on the card.
- **Waiting for approval**: the office hasn't looked yet. Tap **Change it** to fix it, or to delete it with the trash can.
- **Approved, paying you back**: it's approved and you'll be paid back.
- **Paid back**: with the day and how you were paid.
- **Approved**: you used company money, so there's nothing to pay back.
- **Not approved**: with the office's reason.

Once the office approves a receipt you can't change it yourself; ask them.

**Good to know:** you're paid back the way your Staff Pool record says (Zelle, Venmo, check). Only you, the tournament director and admins can see your receipt photos.
