Before an event, your families need three things from you: the player waiver, coach registration and hotel rooms. This page has those messages already written for your club and the event, so all you do is send them.

**Where:** the buttons in your pre-event checklist email open it, one per message. Your club portal shows the same messages.

1. Find the message: **Player waivers**, **Coach registration** or **Hotel rooms**.
2. To change the words, tap **Edit wording**, then **Done**. Keep the long web address on a line of its own: it becomes the link.
3. To send it from your email, tap **Gmail**, **Outlook (web)** or **My mail app**. A draft opens with the subject filled in and the message copied. Click into the message box and paste it (**Ctrl + V**, or **⌘ V** on a Mac).
4. Or tap **Copy with link** and paste the message wherever you reach your families: a group text, a team app or a newsletter.
5. Add your families' addresses yourself and send it.

It goes from you, to your own list. The organizer never emails your families.

**Common problems:**
- The pasted message shows a long web address instead of linked words: copy it again with **Copy with link** and paste it into the message box, not the address line.
- **My mail app** opens whatever your device uses for email: Apple Mail on an iPhone or Mac, usually Outlook on a PC.
- The page won't load: open it again from your latest checklist email, or reply to that email for help.
