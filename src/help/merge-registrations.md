If a club registered twice for the same tournament, merge the two entries into one. Every team, payment, invoice and discount from both is kept.

**Where:** People → Team registrations

**To merge two registrations:**
1. On one of the club's entries click **Merge**.
2. Under **Combine ... with**, choose the other entry. Likely matches are marked "likely duplicate".
3. Under **Keep contact details, pay method and registration date from**, pick which entry's details to keep. Anything different on the other entry is written into the notes.
4. Check **After the merge**: the combined team list, **Invoice**, **Discount**, **Paid** and **Balance**, plus any warnings.
5. Click **Merge these two**.

**Common problems:**
- The **Merge** button only shows when there are at least two registrations.
- **Merge these two** stays disabled until the preview has loaded.
- The merged-away entry shows under **Recently Deleted** as merged and can't be restored; its teams and payments now live on the kept entry.
- Club directors on either entry can open the merged one in their portal afterwards. Merging is also how you clear "Waivers under … are hidden" when the same club registered twice under two directors.
