On game day, check players in from the waiver list as they arrive, and print photo badges for lanyards.

**Where:** People → Player waivers

**To check players in:**
1. Click **Check-in mode**. It stays on for this device until you turn it off.
2. Pick a team, or search for a player.
3. Tap the circle next to each player as they arrive. Tap again to undo.
4. Use **Not here yet** and **Checked in** to see who is missing. The bar shows how many are checked in.

**To check in a scanned player pass:** scanning a player's pass QR opens this page with that player at the top under **Scanned pass**. Click **Check in**, then **Done**.

**If a player isn't on the list:** click **Not on the list?**. A parent can scan the QR code, or use **Share link** or **Open form**, to sign the waiver right then. Tap refresh to see the new entry.

**To clear check-ins between days:** click **Clear**, then tap again to confirm. It clears the selected team, or everyone if no team is picked. Waivers are untouched.

**To print badges:**
1. Click **Print badges** (pick a team first to print only that team).
2. On **Player badges**, wait for "ready to print", then click **Print**. It prints 9 badges per letter page.

**Common problems:**
- **Print badges** only shows when the org's **Player pass** option is on (Forms → Player waiver → Optional fields).
- Archived players are left out of check-in and badges.
