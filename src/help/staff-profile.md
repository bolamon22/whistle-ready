Each staff member has a profile page with their contact details, notes, photo and a history of what they've been paid across tournaments.

**Where:** open it from **People → Staff roster → Pay Summary** by clicking a person's photo. The page shows **Staff Pool** at the top so you can go back to the full list.

**What you'll see:**
- Contact details, **Can Ref** (for refs) and **Pay Method**.
- **Notes**: click **+ Add note** or **Edit** to change them, then save.
- **Payment History**: totals for **Tournaments**, **Games** and **Total Earned**, and a row per payment with **Tournament**, **Games**, **Method**, **Notes**, **Paid** and **Amount**. If nothing has been paid, it says **No payments recorded yet**.

**To change the photo:** click the photo and choose a new image, or click **Remove**.

To edit name, roles, cert level or other details, use **Edit** on the Staff Pool instead. See [Add and edit people in the Staff Pool](/staff).
