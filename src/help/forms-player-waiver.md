The **Forms** page holds your organization's reusable forms. New tournaments copy these as their starting point. This page covers **Player Waiver**: the waiver and optional questions players complete to compete. For **Registration Confirmation**, see "Who gets new-registration emails".

**Where:** Home → **Admin** → **Forms** (admins). Directors can open [Forms](/dashboard/org/forms) directly.

**To edit the player waiver:**
1. Click **Player Waiver** to open it. The public link is shown at the top with **View** and **Copy**.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Change the **Waiver title** and **Waiver text**. The text supports Markdown (## headings, **bold**, - bullets).
4. Under **Optional fields**, tick what to ask: **Gender**, **Player grade**, **Position**, **Team or club name**, **Second parent**, **Home town**, **Hotel / rental question**, **Newsletter opt-in**, **Player pass**.
5. Set the **Confirmation title** and **Confirmation message**, and tick **Email a confirmation to the registrant** if you want one sent.
6. Click **Save**.

**If you turn on Player pass:** players upload a photo and get a credential card with a QR code for check-in.
1. Choose a card style: **Classic**, **Brushed Steel**, **Gold Edition** or **Frost**.
2. Fill in the example card shown on the form. It has its own Save button.
3. Under **Player card · second QR code**, pick what the second code opens: **Tournament event page (on your site)**, **Your Instagram (from Site settings)**, **Your Facebook (from Site settings)**, **Your website** or **Custom link**.

The section footer shows how many submissions you have.

**Common problems:**
- **Cancel** throws away unsaved changes in that section only.
- Leaving the page with unsaved changes shows a browser warning.
- The waiver is public as soon as you save. Players see the new text on their next visit.
