Club directors and coaches use the team registration form to enter one or more teams in a tournament. You can enter all of your club's teams on one form.

**Where:** the **Register a team** button on the event page (/tournaments/<id>/event). The button only shows while team registration is open.

1. Fill in your club details: **Club Name**, **Club Contact**, **Club Contact Email** and **Club Contact Mobile Phone**. **Club Based In**, **Club Website** and **Instagram** are optional. If the form shows "Already on file as ... — same club?", tap **Use that spelling** so your club matches its earlier entries.
2. Under **Create a password for your club portal**, choose a password of at least 8 characters. Your **Club Contact Email** is your login, so your club portal is ready the moment you register. Already have a Whistle Ready login with that email? Enter that password instead.
3. Answer **Will your club need hotel rooms?** (**Yes**, **No** or **Maybe**). If Yes or Maybe, rough estimates for **Hotel**, **Rooms** and **Nights** are fine.
4. Optionally **Upload Logo** under **Club Logo**. It is used for every team unless you **Upload Custom** for a team.
5. Under **Team Information**, fill in each team: **Club Name**, **Team**, **Division**, **Coach Name**, **Coach Phone** and **Coach Email**. Tap **+ Add Team** for each extra team, or **Remove** to drop one.
6. Check the **Estimated Total**. Tap **view fee schedule** to see how the fee is worked out.
7. Pick a **Payment Options** choice: **Bank Transfer (ACH) — No Fee**, **Credit Card**, **PayPal / Venmo**, **Zelle** or **Check**. Card and PayPal / Venmo add a processing fee shown on the form.
8. Add anything else under **Additional Notes**, then tap **Continue to Payment** (bank transfer, card, PayPal / Venmo) or **Submit Registration** (Zelle, check).

**Already signed in?** There's no password box. If the contact email is your own login's email, the registration goes straight into your club portal. If it's someone else's, that person gets an email link to set up their own login. Tournament staff entering a club for them see the same note.

**Common problems:**
- If a division shows "full, waiting list", a team you enter there goes on the waiting list. It is not included in your total and you are not charged for it.
- You must pick a payment option and answer the hotel question before the form will submit.
- The portal password needs at least 8 characters. Forgot the password for a login you already have? Tap **Forgot it?** under the password box; the reset page opens in a new tab so you don't lose the form.
- To change teams or divisions after you register, contact the organizer.
