Auto-assign fills a day's open ref and scorekeeper slots for you. Clear removes assignments in bulk when you need to start over.

**Where:** **Setup → Assigner**. **Auto-assign** is at the top right; **Clear assignments…** is in the **⋯** menu next to it. Pick the day first; both work on the day shown.

**To auto-assign:**
1. Click **Auto-assign**.
2. Under **Fill**, tick what you want filled: **Scorekeepers**, **Boys' refs** and/or **Girls' refs**. Nothing is ticked when the window opens, so you can't fill another assigner's crew by accident.
3. Leave **Keep scorekeepers on one field all day** ticked if each scorekeeper should stay on one field.
4. Click **Preview** to see the picks first, then **Save**, or **Skip the preview and assign now**. It fills open slots using availability and gender, giving each one to whoever has the fewest games so far, and doesn't touch assignments you already made.
5. Read the result: how many slots were filled, **Games needing staff**, and how many more refs or scorekeepers you'd need to fill everything.

**To clear assignments:**
1. Click **⋯**, then **Clear assignments…**.
2. Under **What do you want to clear?**, tick **Girls Referees**, **Boys Referees** and/or **Score Keepers**.
3. Enter **Your name (for the audit log)**.
4. Type **DELETE** in the confirm box and click **Clear**.

**Common problems:**
- Clearing cannot be undone.
- **Preview** stays grayed out until you tick at least one of Scorekeepers, Boys' refs or Girls' refs.
- The **Clear** button stays grayed out until you tick at least one group, enter your name and type DELETE exactly.
- Boys' or girls' game: from the division's name, or, when the name says neither, from the event's **Teams** (picked on **+ New Tournament** or with **Edit** on its card). A game that is still neither can take either kind of ref. **Clear** sorts games the same way.
- Auto-assign follows each person's answers on the **Availability** page: it only puts them on games they said they can work. People who haven't answered count as available, so remind them first.
