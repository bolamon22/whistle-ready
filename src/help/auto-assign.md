Auto-assign fills a day's open ref and scorekeeper slots for you. Clear removes assignments in bulk when you need to start over.

**Where:** **Setup → Assigner**, toolbar at the top. Pick the day first; both buttons work on the day shown.

**To auto-assign:**
1. Click **Auto-assign**.
2. Leave **Keep scorekeepers on one field all day** ticked if each scorekeeper should stay on one field.
3. Click **Run Auto-Assign**. It fills open slots based on availability, gender and game targets, and doesn't touch assignments you already made.
4. Read the result: how many slots were filled, **Games needing staff**, and how many more refs or scorekeepers you'd need to fill everything.
5. Click **Run Again** to retry, or **Close**.

**To clear assignments:**
1. Click **Clear**.
2. Under **What do you want to clear?**, tick **Girls Referees**, **Boys Referees** and/or **Score Keepers**.
3. Enter **Your name (for the audit log)**.
4. Type **DELETE** in the confirm box and click **Clear**.

**Common problems:**
- Clearing cannot be undone.
- The **Clear** button stays grayed out until you tick at least one group, enter your name and type DELETE exactly.
- Auto-assign uses the **Availability** page, so mark people out there first.
