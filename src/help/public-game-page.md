Each game has its own page with the time, field, teams and score. It is handy for sharing one game with family.

**Where:** tap a game number on the schedule page, or a game in the live score ticker at the top of the schedule.

1. The card shows the date, time and field, and a status: **Upcoming**, **In progress**, **Final** or **Canceled**.
2. The score shows once scoring starts. Scores update live during the game.
3. Tap the calendar icon (**Add to calendar**) to add an upcoming game to your phone's calendar.
4. Tap the share icon (**Share game**) to send the link. It may show **Copied!**
5. Tap **Full schedule & standings**, or the tournament name at the top, to go back.

**Common problems:**
- **Game not found** means the game was removed or the link is old. Tap **Back to tournament** and find the game on the schedule.
- A field or time showing TBD has not been set yet. Check back later.
