Before the schedule is built, the organizer may ask each club to check its team list. This page lets a club director confirm the list in one tap or send a correction.

**Where:** the **Review + confirm your teams** button in the "Confirm your teams" email from the organizer.

1. Open the link. The page is titled **Confirm your teams** with your club name, and lists each team with its division.
2. If everything is right, tap **Everything's right — confirm**. You will see **You're confirmed — thank you!**
3. If something changed (a team added, dropped or moved to another division), tap **Something changed — send an update**.
4. Describe the change in **What changed?** and tap **Send change request**. You will see **Change request sent**. Tap **Back** if you change your mind.

**Common problems:**
- **Already confirmed** means you confirmed before. You can still send a change.
- If the page says the organizer made the change you asked for, look over the list again and confirm.
- If the page says **No teams on file yet**, use the change box to tell the organizer which teams you should have.
- Questions: reply to the email that brought you to this page.
- Signed in to your club portal? You can confirm there too, and ask to move or remove a team from your team list.
