When your vendor application is approved, you get an email with a private link. That page shows your status, lets you pay the booth fee, and holds your booth packet.

**Where:** the link in your vendor approval email. Keep it; it's the only way back to this page.

1. Open the link. The banner at the top shows your status: **Under review**, **You're approved**, or **Not this time**.
2. If approved, find **Booth fee** and tap the **Pay** button. You can pay by card or bank transfer. If part is already paid (a deposit by check, say), the page shows **Paid so far** and **Left to pay**, and **Pay** charges only what's left.
3. Need an invoice, for your records or for an accounting office that pays by check? Tap **Download invoice (PDF)**. It says who to make a check out to and where to send it.
4. After paying you'll see **Payment received**. Bank transfers take a few days to clear; your spot is held either way. When it clears, the page shows **Paid in full**, with **Download your paid invoice (PDF)** for your records.
5. Read **Your booth packet** for setup details. If you see **Setup details** instead, booth location and load-in times will be posted here closer to the event.
6. **What you told us** shows the details from your application.

**Common problems:**
- "Could not start checkout. Please try again." Wait a moment and tap **Pay** again.
- **Not this time** means there was no space for this one. Nothing was charged, and you can apply again for a future event.
