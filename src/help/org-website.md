The **Website** page edits your organization's public site while showing it to you. Your site is in the middle, the list of what's on it is on the left, and whatever you click opens on the right.

**Where:** **Organization** → **Website** in the top bar, or **Website** on your home page. From your own site, sign in and click **Edit site** at the bottom left, or point at any part of the page and click its **Edit** button to open the editor right on that part.

**To edit the site:** click a part of the page in the middle, or its name on the left. Change it on the right and the page updates as you type; a dashed outline shows the words you're changing. Nothing changes on your live site until you click **Publish**. The dot next to it reads **Changes not published yet** until you do. Use **View site** to see it live.

**Preview size:** the three buttons at the top show the page as it looks on a computer, a tablet and a phone.

**Sections tab** (the home page, top to bottom):
- **Menu & logo**: **Upload** a logo or pick one **From library**. The menu fills itself.
- **Hero**: **Headline**, **Subtext** and an optional **Background image (optional)**. The Next up tag and the **Register a team** button follow your next tournament by themselves.
- **Upcoming tournaments**, **By the numbers** and **Register band** are marked **Auto**: they fill themselves from your tournaments and results. **By the numbers** has fields such as **Running events since** for the years before your results went online.
- **About**: **Heading** and **Body**.
- **From the fields**: your photo gallery. **Add photos**, then tag them to a tournament, caption and credit them. Use **Select all** with **Apply caption**, **Apply credit** or **Apply tournament** for many at once.
- **Our partners**: click **Add sponsor**, then fill in the name, link, role and logo. Each sponsor is one line: click **Edit** to change it and **Done** when you're finished. Presenting sponsors are listed apart from the partners in the grid; drag the handle or use the arrows to reorder within each group. Under **Shows at**, keep **Every event**, or choose **Only some events** and tick dates under each event. Events coming up and from the past year are listed, and **Show older events** opens the rest. **Every year** keeps the sponsor on that event's next tournament as soon as you create it. **See an event's logo wall** lists the logos an event's page will show, including changes you haven't published. Tick **Ask for sponsors on event pages** to add a sponsor pitch.
- **Instagram**: shows your latest posts. Needs a Business or Creator account.
- **Contact & footer**: **Email**, **Phone**, **Hours**, **Address**, and Facebook, Instagram and website links.

**Move or hide a section:** the arrows next to a section move it up or down the page; the eye hides it (or click **Hide** at the top right). A hidden section shows as a striped bar in the editor so you can bring it back with **Show**.

**Pages tab:** pages like Directions, Hotels or FAQ. Click **New page**, give it a title and write it on the right; the middle shows the page as visitors will see it. Choose **In the menu** or **In the footer**. **Delete** removes a page.

**Events tab:** every tournament's own event page, with **Upcoming** and **Past** tabs. Click **Edit page** to change an event's page, or **View** to see it.

**Log in link:** every page of the site has **Log in** at the top (a person icon on phones) and **Log in to your account** in the footer. Club directors, coaches, parents and staff all use it and land on their own page. Once signed in, the header link reads **My account**. It's always there; there's nothing to turn on.

**Upcoming event logos:** the header of your site and of each event's pages shows the logos of your next four events, soonest first, each linking to its event page. On computers they sit next to **Tournaments**, and pointing at one shows its dates, location and, while registration is open, **Register**. On phones and tablets they're in a row under the menu bar. They come from each tournament's logo and dates, and an event drops off after its last day. There's nothing to turn on.

**Common problems:**
- Nothing is published until you click **Publish**. Leaving the page before that asks you first.
- **Edit site** shows only to directors of this organization, and only once you're signed in on that site.
- Each tournament holds up to 100 gallery photos.
- Changing an info page's address breaks old links to it.
