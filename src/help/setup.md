**Tournament setup** is where you describe the tournament: basics, divisions, venues, fees, registration, pay rates, game timing and the public event page. Settings used to be a separate page; it now opens here.

**Where:** **Setup → Tournament setup**. On a phone, pick the part you want from the **Setup section** dropdown.

**The sections (left side):**
- Basics: **General info**, **Divisions**, **Venues & fields**
- Money: **Team fees**, **Registration types**, **Staff pay rates**
- Play: **Game Timing & Format**, **Standings tiebreakers**
- Public: **Page builder**, **Tournament info**, **Broadcast permissions**

A check next to a section means it has been filled in. The **Summary** box counts divisions, venues, fields and **Sections done**.

**To edit general info:**
1. Open **General info**.
2. Fill in **Tournament Name** (required), **Sport**, **Location**, **Start Date** and **End Date**.
3. Under **Tournament Logo**, click **Upload Logo** (or **Use from library**). **Remove** clears it.
4. Click **Save Changes** (top right; at the bottom on a phone).

One **Save Changes** button saves every section at once, so you can move between sections and save at the end.

**Common problems:**
- Nothing is saved until you click **Save Changes**.
- If only part saves, a message names what did NOT save (for example tournament info or the public event page content). Click **Save Changes** again.
- To place games on fields, use the **Game Scheduler** link under the sections, or **Setup → Scheduler**.
