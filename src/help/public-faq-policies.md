Organizer answers about event-day policies. The event's **Rules** page and the weather policy page have the full text.

**What happens with weather or lightning? Is there a refund?**
Play stops as soon as lightning is seen, thunder is heard or the venue's weather alert goes off. Everyone leaves the fields for a car or a building; dugouts, canopies and open pavilions aren't safe. Play resumes 30 minutes after the last lightning or thunder, and any new strike starts the 30 minutes over. The city or the venue can close the fields, and the tournament can't overrule them. To catch up, games may be shortened (shorter halves, no halftime, a running clock). A game stopped at halftime or later counts, with the score at the stop; one stopped earlier is decided by the Tournament Director. If teams end up with different numbers of games, seeding goes by points per game. In a long delay the event may switch to a festival format. Updates come through Whistle Ready and the tournament website: be at your field ready to play within 15 minutes of the all clear, or the game may be a forfeit. Registration fees are non-refundable, including when weather or a field closure shortens or cancels games. The Weather Policy page has the full text.

**Can a player guest on another team?**
Teams sometimes need to share or borrow players to field a full roster, so guest playing across different divisions is allowed. Please limit double-rostering when you can, to avoid player fatigue and schedule conflicts. To keep competition fair, A-roster players should not guest on B teams. Roster switching is not allowed in playoff games, and all divisions follow USA Lacrosse age guidelines, with no age or grad-year exemptions.

**How are ties in the standings broken?**
In order: overall record, head-to-head result, fewest goals against, goal differential (capped at plus or minus 7 a game), then a coin flip.

**Is there an admission fee? Can we bring coolers or tents?**
There's no admission fee. Pop-up tents, folding chairs and coolers are welcome.

**Can we bring our dog?**
Unfortunately not. We love dogs, but the Village of Wellington doesn't allow dogs at Village Park. Service dogs are welcome.

**Is there a parking fee? Can we bring an RV?**
Parking is free. Unfortunately, because parking at the fields is limited, we can't accommodate RVs.

**Do players need a USA Lacrosse membership?**
Yes.

**Do photographers or videographers need a media pass?**
Yes. Apply for a media credential: on the **Gallery** page tap **Shoot with us**, then **Apply for a credential**. The organizer reviews every application.

**What does it cost to be a vendor, and when do we set up?**
One booth is $600 per event, two booths at opposite ends are $1,000, and a showcase (demos, no sales) is $300. Booth location and load-in and load-out times are sent after you're approved.

**Can food trucks or food vendors come?**
Food and beverage is contracted separately and isn't open to new applications.
