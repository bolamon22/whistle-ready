The gallery holds photos and short video clips from our events, sorted into albums by tournament.

**Where:** **Gallery** in the website menu, or **View gallery** in the "From the fields" section of the home page.

1. Open **Gallery**. If there are several albums, you'll see **All photos** plus one album per event (and sometimes a separate album for video clips).
2. Tap an album to open it. Tap **All albums** to go back.
3. Scroll down and tap **Load more** to see more photos from a large album.
4. Tap a photo to see it full size. Use the arrows to move between photos.
5. To send a photo to someone, tap **Share**. When it says **Link copied**, paste the link into a text or post.
6. Many photos show a credit ("Photo:" and the photographer's name) under them.

**Common problems:**
- **Gallery** only appears in the menu once the organizer has posted photos.
- "No photos yet — check back soon." means that album is still empty.
- Want photos of your own player? See "Book a photographer for your player".
