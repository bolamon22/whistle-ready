**Documents** keeps the tournament's paperwork in one place: grant applications, insurance certificates, permits and venue contracts. It is for staff only.

**Where:** **Setup → Documents**.

**To upload a document:**
1. Pick a category: **Grant**, **Insurance**, **Permit**, **Venue**, **Contract**, **Financial** or **Other**.
2. Click **Upload file** and choose the file (PDF, Word, Excel or an image, up to 10 MB).

Documents are grouped by category.

**To download or delete:** use **Download** or **Delete** on the document.

**Common problems:**
- Deleting asks for confirmation and cannot be undone.
