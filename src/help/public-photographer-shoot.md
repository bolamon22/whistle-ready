Photographers and videographers can apply for a media credential to shoot an event. The organizer reviews every application.

**Where:** a link the organizer shares, at /tournaments/<id>/shoot.

1. Read **What you get** and **Pick how far you want to take it** to see the options.
2. Under **Media credential application**, fill in **Your name**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Portfolio link**. **Business name** is optional. Some events also require your **Instagram handle**.
3. Tick **Where do you post?** and **Which events are you shooting?**
4. Under **What do you want to do?**, pick one or more options.
5. Optional: **Stills or video?**, **Camera and lenses**, **Liability insurance?** and **Anything else**.
6. Read the terms, tick **I have read and agree to the above**, and tap **Send my application**.

**Common problems:**
- The credential preview says **not valid yet** until the organizer approves you.
- You must pick at least one event and one option, and agree to the terms, before sending.
