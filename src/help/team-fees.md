**Team fees** sets what a club pays per team on the public registration form, and the confirmation clubs see and receive after registering.

**Where:** **Setup → Tournament setup → Team fees**.

**To set the per-team price:**
1. Under **Per-team rate by number of teams**, enter the price for each tier. Each tier covers a range of team counts; change where a tier ends by editing its top number.
2. Click **Add tier** for another tier. The **×** removes one (the last tier cannot be removed).
3. Click **Save Changes**.

**To charge some divisions a different rate:**
1. Click **Add division rate**.
2. Give it a **Rate name**, type the text the division names contain (for example "7v7"), and enter the price.

**To add an early-bird discount:**
1. Click **Add early-bird discount**.
2. Enter a label, pick **$ off** or **% off**, the amount, and the **if registered by** date.

**Reset to defaults** puts the pricing back to the starting values.

**To edit the confirmation letter:**
1. Scroll to **Confirmation letter**.
2. Edit **Email subject**, **Welcome message**, **What's next** and **Sign-off**. You can use {club}, {tournament}, {dates}, {location} and {org}; teams, fees and links are added for you.
3. Click **Preview email** to see exactly what the club contact receives (tick **Show as paid online** or **Show as returning club** to try other versions).
4. In **Notify your team**, list the staff addresses (comma-separated) that get an internal heads-up for each new registration.

**Common problems:**
- Any confirmation field left blank uses your org default from **Forms → Registration Confirmation**.
- The confirmation email goes to the club contact on every new registration.
