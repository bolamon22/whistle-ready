Every player must complete the player waiver for the event before they can compete. Parents usually fill it in.

**Where:** the player waiver link from your club or the organizer. On our website it's at /register/player.

1. Open the link. If more than one event is open, you'll see **Which event is your player attending?** Tap your event. If only one is open, the waiver opens straight away.
2. Under **Registering for**, pick your **Club** and then your **Team** from the lists.
3. Fill in **Player information**: **Player full name**, **Date of birth**, **Gender**, **Player grade**, **Home town** and **Home state**. Jersey number, position and a player photo are optional.
4. Fill in **Parent information**: **Parent name**, **Parent mobile phone** and **Parent email**. A second parent and an emergency contact can be added too.
5. Answer whether your family is staying at a hotel or vacation rental.
6. Read the waiver, tick **I have read and agree to the waiver and release of liability above**, and type your full name as your signature.
7. Tap **Submit registration**.

Your player card builds as you type and is ready to save or print when you're done.

**Common problems:**
- Club or team not in the list? Choose **Other / not listed** and confirm **I've checked the list — my team isn't there.** A team typed in by hand isn't connected to the roster, schedule or game-day check-in, so check the list carefully first.
- "No events open right now" means waivers open once an event is scheduled.
- "Please agree to the waiver and sign" means the box or signature is missing.
