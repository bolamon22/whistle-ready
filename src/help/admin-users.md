**Logins** (once called User Management) is the admin list of every account. Add users, change roles, reset passwords, link club directors to their clubs, or delete accounts.

**Where:** **People** → **Logins** in the top bar, or the **Logins** tab on any admin page. Searching a name or email in the top bar and picking it under **Logins** opens this page filtered to that person.

**To add a user:**
1. Click **+ Add login**.
2. Enter **Full Name**, **Email** and a **Password** (at least 6 characters).
3. Pick a **Role**.
4. Click **Add login**. Share the password with them yourself.

**To change a role:** pick a new role in the **Role** column. It saves right away.

**To reset a password:** click **Reset PW**, enter the new password, and click **Reset Password**.

**To pick which staff pay an assigner sees:** under an Assigner's row, click the groups next to **Sees pay for**: **Boys refs**, **Girls refs**, **Scorekeepers**, **Field ops & trainers**. Each click saves. **Default until you pick** means they see boys and girls refs only. Tick all four when one person runs everybody. This sets what they see on **Staff pay**, **Assignments**, the staff roster and the Staff Pool.

**To pick a field director's events:** under a Field Director's row, click the events next to **Works**. Each click saves, and they see only those events. See "Field directors: what they see and do".

**To link a club director or coach to a club:**
1. Click **Clubs** on their row (shown for every role but Parent).
2. Pick a tournament from **Select tournament…**, then pick a club or type a club name.
3. Click **+ Add Link**. Repeat for more clubs, then click **Done**.
Use **Remove** to unlink.

A staff login can direct a club too, like a field director who runs his own club. Keep their role and link the club here. They get **Club portal** in their account menu, show under **Clubs & coaches** too, and someone else posts the scores of their club's games.

A link opens that club's registrations at that tournament as they are when you add it. A registration made later isn't added by name, so the person who registers it, and anyone they add from their portal, can open it. A club with no registration at the tournament yet can't be linked.

**To view a user's details:** click **Profile**.

**To delete a user:** click **Delete** and confirm.

**To find people:**
- Type in **Search people, jobs, clubs…**: a name, email, role, staff job (for example "trainer" or "ref") or club name.
- Click a chip: **Everyone**, **Office** (admins, directors, schedulers, assigners, field directors), **Staff**, **Clubs & coaches** (and anyone linked to a club) or **Parents**. Each shows how many match.
- Under **Staff**, pick a job: **Refs**, **Scorekeepers**, **Athletic trainers**, **Field ops**, or **No staff record**.
- **Last on** narrows to people on now, on in the last 7 or 30 days, or with **No visit recorded**. Visits are recorded from Oct 12, 2026.
- Sort by **Newest first**, **Name A–Z** or **Last on**. **Clear filters** resets.

Every ref, scorekeeper, trainer and field ops worker logs in as **Staff**. Their job comes from their Staff Pool profile with the same email, shown under their name with a **Staff record →** link. **No staff record** means no Staff Pool profile has that email, so the app doesn't know their job: add one in the Staff Pool, or fix the email on either side. Club directors and coaches show the clubs they can open; parents show how many player waivers they're linked to.

**Common problems:**
- Delete cannot be undone. You cannot delete your own account.
- Adding a user here does not add them to an organization. Add them under **Organizations** → the org → **Team & owner**, or have a director invite them from **Your team**.
- The Role menu on the add form may show **Admin** before you pick one. Always choose the role you want.
