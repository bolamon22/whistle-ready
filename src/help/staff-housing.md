Team Housing sends your housing company a regular report of which clubs need hotels, and gives them a private booking board to log bookings. Bookings show up in each event's **Travel & hotels** totals.

**Where:** **Staff** in the top bar, then **Housing**. **People → Travel & hotels** also links here.

**To set up the report:**
1. Under **Housing reports**, fill in **Housing contact** and **Email the report to**. Each field saves when you click out of it.
2. Pick a **Schedule**: **Every Monday, 8:00 AM**, **Mondays + Thursdays, 8:00 AM**, or **Manual — only when I hit Send**.
3. Tick **Include coach contact info** if the housing company should contact clubs directly.
4. Enter the **Club booking link** (the housing company's site where clubs book).

**To share the booking board:** under **His board link**, click **Copy** and send it to the housing company. No login is needed.

**To send a report right away:** click **Send report now**. The page shows when it was last sent.

The live board appears below the settings.

**Common problems:**
- **Send report now** stays grayed out until there is a report email.
- **Rotate** creates a new board link and every old link stops working. It asks you to confirm first.
- Reports are emailed to the housing contact.
