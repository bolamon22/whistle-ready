Exhibit A fills in the county "ISA Background Check Results" table from your tournament's confirmed staff roster, ready to print and attach to the notarized Background Screening and Concussion Affidavit.

**Where:** **People → Staff roster**, then click **Exhibit A**.

**To prepare and print it:**
1. Every confirmed staff member is listed by name. In **Date tests completed**, set each person's background-check date. Each date saves as you pick it.
2. A green shield means the date is good. A warning icon means the date is missing or more than 12 months old.
3. Fix any warnings shown in the yellow box at the top. Counties require re-screening every 12 months.
4. Click **Print**. Only the table prints, with the tournament name and dates at the top.

**Common problems:**
- **No confirmed staff on the roster yet.** Add people on the **Staff roster** first.
- People with a missing or old date print with the **Passed** boxes unchecked, so fix the dates before printing.
