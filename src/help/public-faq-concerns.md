How to raise a concern during the event. Tournament staff don't settle disputes over chat, and Chirp won't judge a referee, team or player.

**I'm a parent, player or fan. Who do I tell?**
Please share your concern with your own coach or club director. Coaches and club directors are the ones who raise concerns with the tournament, so they can look into it with the right information.

**I'm a coach or club director. Who do I tell?**
At the fields, speak to the head official or a tournament staff member, not the referee during the game. Otherwise, email the tournament director at info@sunshinelax.com with your team, the game (field and time) and what happened.

**Can a player play on two teams?**
Players may play on more than one team as long as the teams are in different divisions. Roster switching is not allowed in playoff games.

**What about a player who seems too old?**
All divisions follow USA Lacrosse age guidelines, with no age or grad-year exemptions. If your coach believes a player is ineligible, they should raise it with the tournament director.

**Someone is hurt, or I feel unsafe.**
Find the nearest tournament staff member right away. Call 911 in an emergency.
