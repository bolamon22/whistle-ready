**Print the schedule** gives you the schedule with its officials on paper or as a PDF. **Checkpoints** keep who was on each game, so when the schedule changes you can see who had the games that moved.

**Where:** on the Assigner, open **⋯** (top right), then **Print schedule** or **Checkpoints**. Field directors: **Print schedule** on your home page's event card, or **All days** on the **Field board**. Field directors can print the schedule with its refs and scorekeepers but not change anyone or open Checkpoints.

**To print the schedule (or save a PDF):**
1. Open **⋯ → Print schedule**.
2. Pick **Every day** or one day; **All games**, **Boys games** or **Girls games**; **Refs and scorekeepers**, **Refs only** or **Scorekeepers only**; and **By time** or **By field**.
3. Click **Print**. To get a PDF, pick **Save as PDF** as the printer. It prints landscape, with the event, what you picked and when it was printed at the top. Open spots print as "open".

**Checkpoints:**
- Whistle Ready saves one by itself just before the schedule changes (a game moves, changes teams, is canceled or deleted, or the schedule is re-imported), once officials are assigned. A string of changes gets one checkpoint, taken before the first.
- Click **Save a checkpoint now** to keep one yourself, for example before you release the schedule.
- Pick a checkpoint to see each game that changed since: **Moved**, **New teams**, **Canceled**, **Removed** or **Different officials**, with the day, time, field and officials **Then** and **Now**. **Only games that had officials** is on to start.
- **Print it** prints the schedule with the officials as that checkpoint had them.
- When games that had officials changed since the newest checkpoint, the Assigner shows a yellow note with **See who had them**. Click **×** to hide it for that checkpoint.
- The last 40 checkpoints of each event are kept.

**Common problems:**
- No checkpoints yet: nothing has been assigned, or the schedule hasn't changed since. Save one with **Save a checkpoint now**.
- A re-imported game shows as **Removed** when its division or game number changed in the new file, since that's how a re-imported game is matched to the old one.
