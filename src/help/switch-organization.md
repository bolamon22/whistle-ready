One login can work for more than one organization: an assigner who runs officials for Sunshine Events Group and for his own events, say. You sign in once and switch between them.

**To switch:** click your organization's logo at the top left (it has an arrow when you have more than one) and pick the other one under **Your organizations**. On a phone, tap the menu button: **Your organizations** is at the top. The app reloads in that organization.

**What changes when you switch:**
- Your role is the one that organization gave you. You can be a director in one and an assigner in the other.
- You see that organization's events, Staff Pool, team and pages, and only those. Nothing from one organization shows in the other.
- Each organization's plan decides which pages it has, so the menus can differ.

**Links into the other organization's events** (an email about a game at one of their events, say) switch you there on the way in, then open the page.

**Common problems:**
- No arrow on your logo? You work for one organization. Whoever runs the other one adds you on their **Your team** page, with the email you sign in with.
- An event you expected isn't listed? Check which organization you're in: the logo at the top left shows it.
- A page says you can't open it? Your role or that organization's plan doesn't include it. Ask its director, or an admin.
