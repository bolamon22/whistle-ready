A **Field Director** runs game day at the events you give them. They work with the scorekeepers, athletic trainers and field ops, see the vendors, and send training videos. They see no money, no registrations or waivers, and no refs. Several field directors can work the same event.

**To make someone a field director:**
1. On **Logins** (admins) or **Your team** (directors), set their role to **Field Director**. On **Your team** you can also invite a new person straight in as a Field Director.
2. Under their name, click the events next to **Works**. Each click saves. They see only these events, so with none picked they see nothing.
3. If they're signed in already, the change reaches them within a minute, or when they reload the page.

**What a field director sees:**
- **Their home page:** each of their events, with **Game day**, **Staff**, **Availability**, **Vendors**, **Schedule** (the public schedule and teams) and **Training**.
- **Staff roster:** the scorekeepers, athletic trainers and field ops on the event, with phone and email. They can add people from the Staff Pool, remove them, **Invite Staff** by email and **Copy signup link**. They can't edit Staff Pool details, and they see no pay.
- **Availability** and **Staff applications** for those jobs. Applicants who only want to referee are left out.
- **Game day:** field requests, incidents, the checklist, the staff directory, **Post scores** and the scorekeeper.
- **Vendor requests:** who's coming and how to reach them. No fees, payments or booth links. Only a director approves or declines a vendor.
- **Training videos:** send the staff videos to their scorekeepers, athletic trainers and field ops, and see who has watched.

**Common problems:**
- "No events yet" on their home page means nobody picked their events next to **Works**.
- Opening a staff page of another event sends them back to their home page. Public event pages still open, as they do for anyone.
- Field directors can't create events, or move or delete games. They can post scores at their own events.
