Two small sections in Tournament setup control what parents see under **Info** on the public schedule page and which staff can post announcements.

**Where:** **Setup → Tournament setup → Tournament info** and **Broadcast permissions**.

**To add game-day info (parking, medical, lost and found):**
1. Open **Tournament info** and click **+ Add section**.
2. Pick an icon, type a **Section title** and the **Details**.
3. Add as many sections as you need; the **×** removes one.
4. Click **Save Changes**. Use **View it** to open the public page.

Until you save any sections, the public page shows sensible defaults.

**To choose who can post announcements:**
1. Open **Broadcast permissions**.
2. Tick the roles allowed to post: **Schedulers**, **Assigners**, **Staff (field ops, medical, etc.)**, **Scorekeepers** or **Referees**. The **Tournament Director** is **Always allowed**.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

Allowed staff get a Broadcast page to post to the public announcement banner. You can remove any post.
