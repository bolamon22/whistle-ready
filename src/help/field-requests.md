Field requests are quick messages to staff on game day, like "Yellow balls needed on Field 5" or "Trainer needed on Field 7". Anyone on staff can post and respond.

**Where:** **Live → Communications**, **Field Request** tab, or **Field Request** on your home page.

**To send a request:**
1. Type the field in **Field #**.
2. Tap a quick request: **Trainer needed**, **Balls — yellow**, **Balls — white**, **Water needed**, **Ref needed** or **No team at field**. Or type your own.
3. Under **Send to**, pick **All staff**, **Field Ops**, **Medical**, **Refs**, **Scorekeepers** or **Assigners**. Quick requests pick a group for you.
4. Tap **Send**.

**For a medical request:** pick **Medical**, set **Urgency** (**Low**, **Medium** or **High**), and describe the injury. For emergencies, call 911 first.

**To answer a request:** tap **Respond** on it. Your name shows as on the way. Tap again to undo.

The **Recent** list refreshes by itself. A request nobody has answered shows **No one has responded yet**. Use the trash icon to remove one.

**Common problems:**
- **Send** stays grayed out until you type a message.
