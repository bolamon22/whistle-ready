When a division is marked **Full**, teams can still register for it, but they go on the waiting list. A waitlisted team is not invoiced and is kept out of pools and games until you give it a spot.

**Where:** Setup → Tournament setup → **Divisions** (to mark a division full) and People → Team registrations (to manage teams)

**To mark a division full:**
1. In Tournament setup open **Divisions**.
2. In the per-division list under **Registration status**, set the division's **Status** to **Full**.
3. Click **Save Changes**. New teams registering for it go on the waiting list.

**To see who's waiting:**
- On Team registrations, the amber **Waiting list** tile shows how many teams are waiting. Click it (or the **Waiting list** button next to the filters) to show only the clubs with a team on the list. Click again to show everyone.
- Each of those clubs has a **Waiting list:** line under its name, listing the team and division. Open the card to see a **Waiting list** tag on the team itself.
- The CSV export has a **Waiting list** column (Yes for waitlisted teams).

**To put a team on or off the waiting list by hand:**
1. In Team registrations click **Edit** on the club.
2. Tick or untick **On the waiting list** under the team. A waitlisted team is not invoiced but still counts toward the club's per-team rate.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

**When you take a team off the list:**
1. The invoice goes up by that team at the club's rate.
2. A box says "A spot opened for..." with a tick for **Email ... the good news with their updated invoice and pay link when I save**. Leave it ticked to tell the club, or untick it if you were only fixing a mistake.
3. Click **Save Changes**. A message confirms whether the "spot opened" email was sent.

**To push waitlisted clubs to pay:** use **Email clubs → Payment reminder → Waiting list**.

**Common problems:**
- Marking a division **Full** does not close registration; it only changes billing for new teams.
- A waitlisted team can't be placed in a pool in **Divisions & teams** until you take it off the list here.
