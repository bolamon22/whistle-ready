Whistle Ready's **Terms of Use** live at **/terms**. They are an agreement with Whistle Ready LLC, which owns the software. They cover the license to use it, keeping non-public screens confidential, and not using it to build a competing product.

**Signing in:** the first time anyone signs in after the terms change, a **We updated our Terms of Use** box appears. Tick **I agree to the Whistle Ready Terms of Use** and press **Agree and continue**. It is asked once per version. **Sign out** leaves without agreeing. The box never appears on the Terms, Privacy or SMS pages, so the **Read the Terms of Use** link can be opened from it.

**Team registration:** the form has a required **I agree to the Whistle Ready Terms of Use** box above the submit button. A club contact who ticks it is not asked again when they first sign in to their club portal with the same email.

**Player waiver:** the waiver box reads **I have read and agree to the waiver and release of liability above, and to the Whistle Ready Terms of Use**.

**Coach waiver:** the line under the signature says signing also agrees to the **Terms of Use**.

Each agreement is saved with the date, the terms version and where it was given, so there is a record of who agreed to what.
