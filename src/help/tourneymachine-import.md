If your teams are in TourneyMachine, export them and import them into a Whistle Ready tournament. Only team registration data comes across: clubs, teams, coaches and divisions. Schedules, scores and pools do not.

**Step 1, export from TourneyMachine:**
1. Log into TourneyMachine and open your event.
2. Go to **Teams**, then **Quick Report**.
3. Download as CSV or Excel. Include as many columns as you can; extra columns are skipped.

**Step 2, import into Whistle Ready:**
1. Open your tournament's **Registrations** page and click **Import teams**.
2. Drop your file on **Drop your spreadsheet here**, or click to browse (.csv or .xlsx).
3. Check **Map Columns**. Whistle Ready guesses the matches, such as **Club Name** and **Team Name**.
4. Review **Preview Import**. Each club becomes one registration with its teams listed under it.
5. Click the **Import** button.

After import, the registrations appear in the team registrations list, where you can edit each one. For more on the import screen, see "Import teams from a spreadsheet".

**Common problems:**
- Importing the same file twice creates duplicate registrations. Check the list before re-importing.
- If no club name column is mapped, the team name is used as the club name.
