Everyone on the tournament's staff roster is available by default. Use this page to mark who can't work a whole day or certain start times, so you can plan around them when assigning games.

**Where:** **People → Staff roster**, then the **Availability** tab.

**To mark someone out for a whole day:**
1. Pick the day from the tabs at the top.
2. Click **All Day Out** in their row (on a phone, tap **All day out** on their card). Their row turns red and shows **Out**.
3. Click it again to make them available.

**To block certain times only:**
1. Pick the day.
2. Click the start times they can't work. Each blocked time shows an X, and the person shows **Partial**.
3. Click a blocked time again to free it.

**What the labels mean:**
- **Available**: can work all day (the default).
- **All Day Out**: can't work any of that day.
- **Partial**: out for the specific times marked.

**Common problems:**
- **No rostered staff.** Add people on the **Staff roster** first.
- **Import games first to see time slots.** The times come from the schedule, so the grid is empty until games exist.
