Everyone on the event's staff roster tells you which days they can work from their staff home: **All day**, **Morning** (games that start before noon), **Afternoon** (games at noon or later), certain game times, or **Can't work**. Every answer is saved, so this page shows who has answered and who hasn't.

**Where:** **People → Staff roster**, then the **Availability** tab.

**At the top:**
- **Answered every day**: how many people have answered for every day of the event.
- **No answer yet**: how many still haven't, and when the last reminder went out. **Show them** lists just them.
- **Ref coverage**: for each morning and afternoon, how many refs can work at the busiest game time against the ref spots those games need. **Short** means you need more refs; the note says how many refs haven't answered yet. A day with no games yet shows **No schedule yet**.

**To remind the people who haven't answered:**
1. Click **Email the 6 who haven't answered** (the number is how many haven't).
2. Untick anyone you want to leave out. Someone with no email on file (text them instead) or outside your staff groups can't be ticked.
3. Check **What they get**, then click **Send**. Each email has one button that opens their days on their staff home.

To remind one person, click **Remind** in their row.

**To set a day for someone who texted you:**
1. Click their answer for that day (or **No answer**).
2. Pick **All day**, **Morning**, **Afternoon** or **Can't work**, or **Pick game times…** to tick the start times they can work.

They see it on their staff home marked **Set by** your name, and can still change it.

**To narrow the list:** **Everyone**, **No answer yet**, **Can't work a day** or **Part of a day**; a button for each job; the **All staff** list (boys or girls refs); and **Find a person**.

**What the colors mean:**
- Green **All day**: any game that day.
- Amber **Morning**, **Afternoon** or times like **8–11 AM**: only those games.
- Red **Can't work**: not that day.
- Dashed **No answer**: hasn't answered yet.

**Good to know:**
- Auto-assign only puts people on games they said they can work. People who haven't answered are still treated as available.
- Staff can pick exact game times once the schedule is published.
- Days marked out on the old grid show as **Can't work**, or **Out** at certain times, until the person answers.

**Common problems:**
- **No rostered staff.** Add people on the **Staff roster** first.
- **No schedule yet** under Ref coverage. Post that day's games first.
