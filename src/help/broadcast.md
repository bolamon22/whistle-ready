A broadcast is an announcement that shows in the banner on the public page, and can alert the phones following those teams.

**Where:** **Live → Communications → Broadcast**. Directors can also use **Broadcast** on their home dashboard.

**To send a broadcast:**
1. Under **Audience**, pick who it is for: **Everyone**, **Division**, **Team**, **Coaches** or **Staff**. For a division or team, choose which one.
2. Type the message, or start from one of the **Quick templates** (weather delay, play resumed, sideline reminder, championship photos).
3. Turn on **Mark urgent** for anything people must see now.
4. Leave **Alert followers’ phones** on to push it to phones following the teams it covers. **This goes to** shows who will hear it.
5. Click **Send broadcast**.

**To remove a broadcast:** find it under **Recent broadcasts** and click **Remove**.

**Common problems:**
- **Coaches** and **Staff** broadcasts are tagged on the public banner but do not alert phones, because coaches and staff are not followers.
- Which staff roles can post is set in **Setup → Tournament setup → Broadcast permissions**. The director always can.
- Phone alerts only reach people who followed a team on the public page and turned alerts on.
