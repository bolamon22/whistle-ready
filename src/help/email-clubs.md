The **Email clubs** dialog sends a letter to each club's contact (the team director), filled in per club. Use it for payment reminders, waiver pushes, schedule announcements, team confirmations and account setup.

**Where:** People → Team registrations → **Email clubs** (or **Email** on one club's row)

**To send a letter:**
1. Pick the letter: **Player waiver reminder**, **Schedule is ready**, **Confirm your teams**, **Payment reminder**, **Set up your account** or **Pre-event checklist**.
2. Tick the clubs under **Send to**. **All** and **None** select everything shown. Clubs with **no email** can't be ticked.
3. Edit the **Subject** and **Letter**. Words like {contact}, {club} and {event} fill in per club. Check the **Preview**.
4. Choose **Send now** or **Schedule** and pick a date and time.
5. Click **Send to N clubs** (or **Schedule for N clubs**).

**To send pay reminders:**
1. Pick **Payment reminder**. The list opens on **Owes**; switch to **Paid** or **All** if needed. Each club shows what it owes.
2. Pick **Standard** for the everyday ask, **Waiting list** for a full event (it says unconfirmed spots get reassigned), or **Urgent** for the last push before the event. **Urgent** lists what each club still has open: balance, player registrations, coach registrations, final team confirmation and account setup (their club login). Anything a club has already done is checked off, and each open item has its own button. It also warns that the spot can go to a team on the waiting list.
3. Each email includes the invoice, paid and balance table and a **Pay online** button.

**To write your own letter:**
1. Click **New letter**.
2. Under **Write it with AI**, describe what the email should say (type it, or click the microphone and say it), then pick the **Button** (**None**, **Player waiver**, **Coach registration**, **Schedule**, **Confirm teams** or **Pay balance**) and the **Tone**. Click **Write it**. Or skip the AI and type the **Subject** and **Letter** yourself.
3. Check the **Preview**, give it a **Name**, and click **Save as a new letter**. It joins the other letters, ready to send now or schedule.

A **Pay balance** letter works like the payment reminders: it goes only to clubs that owe, with the invoice and the **Pay online** button.

For a coach letter, pick **Coach registration**: it puts the coach registration link under the letter. {coachCounts} lists each of the club's teams with the coaches who have registered for it, by name, or "no coach registered yet".

**To rewrite any letter:** under **Letter**, click **More urgent**, **Friendlier**, **Shorter** or **Fix grammar**, or click **Tell it what to change…** and type or say it. **Undo the AI change** puts the old wording back.

The AI writes the words only. Names, balances, team lists and links fill in from each club's record when it sends. A fill-in it doesn't recognize, like {payment_link}, shows in red, and the letter can't be sent or saved until you fix it. You can still send yourself a test.

**To keep your wording:** click **Save as default**. The **Payment reminder** letters are saved separately. Your own letters use **Save changes**, and **Delete letter** removes one.

**To cancel a scheduled send:** open **Email clubs**, find it under **SCHEDULED** and click **Cancel**. The **Email clubs** button shows how many are queued.

**Waiver counts:** expand a club to see **Waivers** per team and "waivers completed" for the club, then click **Send a waiver reminder**.

**Common problems:**
- These emails go to clubs right away when you click **Send**.
- **Send to 0 clubs** means no club is ticked. Click **All** above the list. A payment letter unticks clubs that owe nothing, and **Set up your account** unticks clubs already in the portal; they're ticked again when you pick a letter for every club.
- Clubs already paid are skipped by payment reminders. **Set up your account** skips registrations someone can already open in the club portal; a contact who has a login but whose registration isn't on it yet still gets it, and links it with their existing password.
- The **Pay reminder** column on each row shows the last reminder date.
