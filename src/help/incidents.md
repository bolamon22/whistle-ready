Incidents is a shared log of medical, safety, facility and weather issues so the team can track them until they're handled. For emergencies, call 911 first.

**Where:** **Live → Communications**, **Incidents** tab, or **Incidents** on your home page.

**To log an incident:**
1. Pick the **Type**: **Medical**, **Safety**, **Facility**, **Weather** or **Other**.
2. Pick the **Severity**: **Low**, **Medium** or **High**.
3. Enter the **Field / location (optional)**.
4. Describe it under **What happened**.
5. Tap **Log incident**.

**To close one out:** tap **Resolve** on the incident. It moves to the resolved list. Tap **Show resolved** to see past incidents.

**Common problems:**
- **Log incident** stays grayed out until **What happened** is filled in.
- The trash icon deletes an incident for good.
