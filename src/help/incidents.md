Incidents is a shared log of medical, safety, facility and weather issues so the team can track them until they're handled. For emergencies, call 911 first.

**Where:** **Live → Communications**, **Incidents** tab, or **Incidents** on your home page.

**To log an incident:**
1. Pick the **Type**: **Medical / injury**, **Safety**, **Facility**, **Weather** or **Other**.
2. Pick the **Severity**: **Low**, **Medium** or **High**.
3. Enter the **Field / location (optional)**.
4. Describe it under **What happened**.
5. Tap **Log incident**.

**Injury report (Medical / injury):** the form adds an **Injury report** section.
1. Pick **Who was hurt**: **Player**, **Coach**, **Staff** or **Spectator**.
2. Enter the **Name** (required for a player), **Jersey #**, **Team** and **Game #**.
3. Pick the **Injury** and check the **Time**.
4. Check **Suspected concussion** if there are any signs. The player comes out and can't return today without written clearance from a doctor.
5. Fill in **Treatment given** and the **Outcome**, and whether a **Parent or guardian** was told (with their name and phone).
6. Tap **Save injury report**.

**Follow-ups:** tap **Follow-up** on an injury to add a dated note, such as a call to a parent, and update the outcome.

**Who sees injury details:** only athletic trainers, tournament directors and the person who logged it. Everyone else sees that a medical incident happened, without the details.

**Injury log:** trainers and directors can tap **Injury log (spreadsheet)** to download every injury for the event.

**To close one out:** tap **Resolve** on the incident. It moves to the resolved list. Tap **Show resolved** to see past incidents.

**Common problems:**
- The save button stays grayed out until **What happened** is filled in, and for a player, the **Name**.
- The trash icon deletes an incident for good.
