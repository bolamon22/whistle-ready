Final scores, standings and brackets from past tournaments stay on our website after the weekend, and a "By the Numbers" page sums up our whole history.

**Where:** the website menu, under **More**, then **Results** or **By the Numbers**. From the home page you can also use **Past tournament results** below the event list, or **See the full history**.

1. Open **Results**. Every past event is shown as a card.
2. Narrow the list with the filters at the top: **Tournament** (pick an event series) and **Year**. Tap **All** to reset one filter, or **Clear filters** to reset both.
3. Tap an event's **Results & standings** to see its scores, standings and brackets.
4. For totals across all years, tap **See the whole history by the numbers** (or open **By the Numbers** from the menu). It shows tournaments run, games played, team entries, champions crowned and seasons.
5. From **By the Numbers**, **Browse every result** takes you back to the results list, and **Register a team** opens registration for the next event when it's open.

**Common problems:**
- "No tournaments match those filters." Tap **Clear filters**.
- Some older years on **By the Numbers** are marked as estimated; those totals are approximate.
