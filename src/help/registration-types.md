**Registration types** turns team registration and individual player registration on or off for this tournament.

**Where:** **Setup → Tournament setup → Registration types**. The **Registration open** / **Registration closed** pill on the **Dashboard** opens this section too.

**To open or close team registration:**
1. Tick or untick **Team registration**.
2. Click **Save Changes**.

When it is on, the section says **Open** and shows the registration link. When it is off, it says **Closed — the Register button is hidden from the public page**.

**To offer individual player registration:**
1. Tick **Individual player registration**. Its link appears underneath.
2. Fill in the **Registration description** (what is included).
3. Under **Fee tiers**, enter a **Tier name**, **Price** and optional **Description**, then click **+ Add tier**. **Remove** deletes a tier.
4. Under **Positions** and **Jersey / shorts sizes**, type each option and click **+ Add**.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

**Common problems:**
- Closing team registration hides the **Register** button; it does not cancel teams already registered.
- A division status of **Full** (in the **Divisions** section) is only a badge; it does not stop sign-ups. Turning off **Team registration** does.
