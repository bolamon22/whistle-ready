Organizer answers to the questions clubs ask most about registering. Event-specific dates are on each event page.

**When is the registration deadline?**
Registration closes on the date shown on the event page. Divisions fill early, so register now. You can add or drop teams later.

**Is there still room?**
If registration is still open on the event page, there is room, and registering takes under five minutes. If your division is sold out, sign up for the waitlist. Spots can open up, and there's still a good chance we can get your team in.

**Can we add a team late?**
We accept late additions as long as open spots remain, but divisions fill up and sell out quickly, so space is limited. If your division is already sold out, join the waitlist; we always do our best to get teams in when a spot opens. Adding a team during tournament week is very difficult and can't be guaranteed. To check availability or ask about the waitlist, email info@sunshinelax.com as soon as possible.

**How do we add more teams after registering?**
Register the extra teams with the same registration form and we'll merge them with your club.

**Can you register our teams for us?**
Register directly online; it's the fastest way to secure your spot. If you run into problems, email your full team list to info@sunshinelax.com and we'll help.
