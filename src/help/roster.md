The Staff Roster is the list of people confirmed to work this tournament. Only people on the roster show up in the Assigner, the **Availability** page and the staff directory, and only they are asked which days they can work.

**Where:** **People → Staff roster**. Tabs across the top also open **Availability**, **Time Entries** and **Pay Summary**.

**To add people:**
1. Scroll to **Available to Add**. This lists everyone in your Staff Pool who is not on this event yet.
2. Narrow it with **Search name, email or phone…**, **Role**, **Cert** and **Can ref**. Each row shows the person's phone and email, or **No email** when there isn't one.
3. Click **+ Add** next to each person. They move up to **Confirmed**.

**To remove someone:** click **Remove** on their row under **Confirmed**. This takes them off this tournament only.

**Ref levels:** the red, yellow or green **L1**, **L2** or **L3** next to a ref's cert is their ref level, your assigners' 1-to-3 ranking (3 is the highest). Set it under **Ref level** when you click **Edit**, or for several refs with **Ref level** in the bar. Only directors, admins and assigners see levels. See [Add and edit people in the Staff Pool](/staff).

**To edit someone:** click **Edit** on their row, change the details, and save. Click **Profile** to see their details without editing. Changes here update their Staff Pool record.

**Their other events and training:** **Profile** lists every tournament they're working (this one is marked **this event**), with **Add to an event…** to put them on another. It also shows which training videos they've watched, and **Send the N not watched** emails them to just that person. The **Training** column shows how many they've watched.

**To change many people at once:** tick them, choose a field from **— choose field to edit —**, pick the value, and click **Apply to Selected**.

**Other buttons on this page:**
- **Invite Staff** emails one person an invite to set up their profile.
- **Copy signup link** copies your public staff signup link to paste into your own email.
- **Exhibit A** opens the background-check table for the county affidavit. See [Print the Exhibit A background-check table](/tournaments/*/roster/exhibit-a).

**Common problems:**
- **Remove** happens right away with no confirmation.
- If **Available to Add** is empty, the person isn't in your Staff Pool yet. Add them under **People** → **Staff Pool** in the top bar first.
