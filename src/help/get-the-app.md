Our tournaments run on Whistle Ready. You can do everything right on the website: see your schedule, follow your teams and get tournament details. There's also a mobile version you can put on your phone. You add it to your Home Screen once from your browser, and it opens full screen like any other app, with alerts.

**iPhone:**
1. Open the event's schedule page (or your staff or parent home page) in **Safari**. It has to be Safari for alerts to work.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open Whistle Ready from the new icon on your Home Screen.
4. Tap **Follow** on your team, then **Turn on alerts**, and tap **Allow** when your phone asks.

**Android:**
1. Open the page in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the menu (three dots), then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Open it from the new icon, tap **Follow** on your team, then **Turn on alerts** and **Allow**.

Once alerts are on, you hear when the schedule posts, when a game moves, and the scores for the teams you follow, and nothing else. Staff get their game-day alerts the same way.
