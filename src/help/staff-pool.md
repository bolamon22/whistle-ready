The Staff Pool is your organization's master list of everyone who works your events: refs, scorekeepers, athletic trainers and field operations. Each tournament's roster is picked from this pool.

**Where:** **Staff** → **Staff Pool** in the top bar (on a phone, open the menu, then **Staff Pool**).

**To add one person:**
1. Click **+ Add Staff**.
2. Fill in **Name** (required), **Email** and **Phone**.
3. Under **Roles (select all that apply)**, tick every job they do. For refs, set **Cert Level** and **Can Ref** (**Boys & Girls**, **Boys only** or **Girls only**).
4. Tick **Also serves as Assigner** if they assign games, add **Notes**, then click **Add Staff**.

**To edit someone:** find them with **Search by name…** or the **Role**, **Can Ref** and **App** filters, then click **Edit**. Click **Profile** to see their details without editing. The list is A–Z by last name; click **Name** to reverse it.

**To see someone's events and add them to one:** click **Profile**. Under **Events** are the tournaments they're on, with how many games they have at each. Pick a tournament from **Add to an event…** to put them on its staff roster. Click the **×** on an event to take them off it. **Show past events** lists the ones that are over.

**Training videos:** their **Profile** also shows which of their training videos they've watched, and **Send the N not watched** emails them just to that person. The **Training** column shows how many they've watched, for example **3/11**. See "Send training videos and see who watched".

**Ref levels:** your assigners' own ranking of their refs, from **Level 1** (red, the lowest) to **Level 2** (yellow) and **Level 3** (green). It's separate from **Cert Level** (youth, high school, college), which sets pay.
- To set one, click **Edit** and pick it under **Ref level**. For several refs at once, tick them and choose **Ref level** in the bar.
- The **Level** column shows each ref's red, yellow or green **L1**, **L2** or **L3**, and you can sort by it. **Ref level: all** finds the refs at one level, or **Refs not ranked**.
- Only directors, admins and assigners see levels, and an assigner sees and sets them only for the staff groups they run. Refs never see their own.

**W-9s:** staff upload their own on their staff portal. **W-9 ✓** under **Pay Method** means one is on file; click **Profile** and then **View W-9 →** to open it. Only directors and admins can open a W-9.

**To change many people at once:**
1. Tick the box next to each person.
2. In the bar that appears, pick a field from **— choose field to edit —** (for example **Role**, **Cert Level** or **Ref level**), pick the new value, and click **Apply to Selected**.

**To add several people to a tournament:** tick them, pick the tournament from **Add to event…** in the bar, and confirm. Anyone already on it is left as they were.

**To import a spreadsheet:**
1. Click **Bulk Import**, then choose **Refs** or **Other Staff**.
2. Click **Upload File** (.xlsx, .xls or .csv).
3. Under **Map your columns**, match each field to a column in your file. For the name, map **Full name**, or **First name** and **Last name** (columns with those names are matched for you, and a name written "Smith, John" comes in as John Smith). Check the preview, then click **Import** (the button shows how many staff it will add).
4. Click **Pool** to go back to the list.

**To clear out old profiles with no email:**
1. Click **N without email** next to the counts above the list. It only shows when there's someone to clear out.
2. Under **Past events to treat as samples**, keep ticked the past events whose history doesn't matter. Anyone on an upcoming event is always kept.
3. Profiles with no email and no phone are ticked for you. **Phone but no email** starts unticked: tick the ones to remove, and leave anyone you still use.
4. Click **Delete N profiles** and confirm. This can't be undone.

**Common problems:**
- **Unassign** on this page deletes the person from your Staff Pool entirely, not just from one event. To take someone off a single tournament, use **Remove** on that tournament's **Staff roster** instead.
- The **App** filter shows who has a login: **Registered**, **Invited**, **Not invited** or **No email**. See [Invite staff to the app](/staff).
