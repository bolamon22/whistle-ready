The Assigner is where you put refs and scorekeepers on each game. Every game has ref slots (1 to 3) plus one scorekeeper slot.

**Where:** **Setup → Assigner**.

**To assign staff on the grid:**
1. Pick the day from the day buttons at the top left (the number is how many games it has).
2. Click **Staffing** (at the right end of the second row) to open the staffing panel. Its **Staff** tab lists everyone on the tournament roster. Use **Find a name**, filter **All**, **Boys**, **Girls** or **SK**, and click **Fewest games first** to switch to **A–Z** (by last name). The number after a name is how many games they have that day. A red, yellow or green **L1**, **L2** or **L3** is the ref's level (3 is the highest), which you set on the Staff Pool; only directors, admins and assigners see it.
3. Drag a name onto a game's slot, or click the slot and pick a name. The pick list runs A–Z by last name and shows it last name first ("Cox, Chris"); typing a first or last name finds them.
4. To change how many refs a game needs, use the minus and plus buttons on the game. To set every game that day at once, open **Layout** (the sliders button next to **Card size**) and click **1 ref**, **2 refs** or **3 refs**.

**The staffing panel:** it stays out of the way until you click **Staffing**. The panel then slides in over the board (the buttons at the top move over so they stay in reach) with three tabs: **Staff**, **Needs** and **Staff portal**. Click **Staffing** again, **»**, or the Esc key to close it. The pin button keeps it open beside the board instead (on a wide screen; remembered on this browser).

**Reading the grid:**
- Colors show **Full crew**, **A slot open**, or **Nobody, or double-booked**.
- The top of the page shows how many slots are filled for the day. A red **double-booked** count appears when someone is on two games at once; click it to see just those games.
- Filter the games by search, division, **All** / **Boys** / **Girls** (Boys turns blue and Girls pink when on; click it again for All), and **Needs crew** or **Conflicts**. The numbers are that day's games. Games that don't match fade on the grid. **Clear** resets the filters.
- **Card size** (**Full**, **Compact**, **Mini**) changes how much each game shows. **Full** cards have score boxes only when the event is set to **Show scores** (pick **Show scores** or **No scores** on **+ New Tournament** or **Edit** on its card).
- **Everyone**, **Refs** and **Scorekeepers** pick which slots every game shows. **Refs** or **Scorekeepers** shows just those people, and **Needs crew** and each time's "crewed" count only them. **Compact** cards also hide the teams then; switch to **Full** to see them. For a view-only copy to use at the fields, open **Live → Field board**.
- **Layout** holds **Fit all fields** or **Fixed width** columns, and **Fold staffed**, **Fold all** and **Open all** to collapse time rows.
- The staffing panel's **Needs** tab shows who is on games that day and **Min staff (busiest day)**, an estimate of how many refs and scorekeepers you need based on **Games/ref**. Click **By day** for each day.

**Other views:** **List**, **Division** and **Staff** (pick a name under **View schedule for:** to see one person's games). In the list view, click a game to open **Assign Staff** and pick names for each slot.

**What staff can see (Staff portal):** the **Staff portal** tab of the staffing panel (open **Staffing**, then **Staff portal**) shows, for **Boys officials**, **Girls officials** and **Scorekeepers**, whether they can see their games yet. Until a group is shown, its games are only on this board: they aren't on the officials' staff home or My games, and nobody is told. Build and fix the schedule first, then click **Show to staff** (this emails nobody) or send **Notify officials**, which shows that group's games as it sends. **Hide** takes them off the staff portal again; nothing on the board changes. A new event starts with every group hidden.

**Someone can't work (take one person off):**
1. Click **⋯** (More actions), then **Clear assignments…**, then **One person**.
2. Start typing their name and pick them. You see every game they're on.
3. Choose **The whole event** or **Just** the day you're on.
4. Keep **Also mark them as can't work** ticked so Auto-assign doesn't put them back on and Availability shows it.
5. Type your name for the audit log, click **Take … off N games…**, then **Yes, clear them**. Their slots go back to open.

**Tell your officials (Notify officials):**
1. When the assignments are ready, click **Notify officials** at the top of the Assigner.
2. Under **Who**, tick **Boys officials**, **Girls officials** or **Scorekeepers** (any mix; nobody is ticked until you pick). Each group's own assigner can send to just their group. A group that is still hidden is shown on the staff portal when you send. To email only some of them, pick **Only people I pick** and tick names (search, **All**, **None**); the group's games still become visible on the staff portal.
3. Choose **Every day of the event** or **Just** the day you're on, add an optional **Note** (for example where to check in), and check the count.
4. Keep **Ask them to confirm their games** ticked to have them confirm, and set **Confirm by**. Each email then gets a **Confirm my games** button that needs no login. Untick it to just send their games with an **Open My games** button.
5. Click **Send to N officials**. Each official gets an email with only their own games (day, time, field, game, teams and whether they're a referee or scorekeeper). Anyone who turned on Whistle Ready alerts gets a phone alert too.
6. After you make changes, send again with **Only officials whose games changed since the last send** ticked, so everyone else isn't emailed twice. Games that moved after someone confirmed them need confirming again.
7. To see the email first, click **Email me a test**. It sends you the email the official with the most games (or the person you picked) would get, marked **Test**. Nobody else gets anything, nothing becomes visible, and its buttons only open My games.

**Changes after you've sent games:** once a group is shown under **Staff portal** and its officials have been sent **Notify officials**, Whistle Ready tells them on its own, by email and phone alert, the moment one of their games moves, is canceled or brought back, or they're put on or taken off a game. When the scheduler or a director moves a game, the assigner who sent that group its games is told too; your own changes go to the official only. Before a group is shown, changes go to nobody.

**Who confirmed:** **Confirmed N/N** next to **Auto-assign** opens the Confirmations page. On the board, a **✓** after a name means they confirmed that game. If an official can't work a game, it comes off their schedule, you get an email, and the slot shows **open** with their name (for example **open · Brooks can't**). See "See which officials confirmed their games".

**To rename a division:** click the **⋯** button at the top right of the Assigner, then **Divisions** (the Head Assigner, directors and schedulers). Change the name and click **Rename**: every game in the division moves with it. The tag under each name shows whose game it is, **Boys**, **Girls** or **Boys & girls**, from the name, or from the event's **Teams** when the name says neither, so adding Boys or Girls to a name changes it. A name another division already has is refused.

**Games are the scheduler's:** assigners can change **refs per game** but can't add, import, move, edit or delete games, rename divisions or enter scores. Ask the scheduler or director (the Head Assigner on the Assigner plan). To bring games in, see "Import, build or delete the games".

**To prevent accidental changes:** click **⋯**, then **Lock editing**. A **Locked** tag shows at the top; click it to unlock. This applies on your own browser only.

**Common problems:**
- Assigning someone already on a game at the same time asks you to confirm before it double-books them.
- Only people on the tournament's **Staff roster** appear here.
- In a game's edit box, **Cancel game** takes the game out of the event: it stays on the board marked **Canceled**, stops counting as a game for both teams, and leaves the public schedule. **Restore game** brings it back. To just close the box, click **Close**. To take a game off the schedule for now, use **Park**.
- Assigning a game doesn't tell the official, and they can't see it until their group is shown under **Staff portal**. Use **Notify officials** when you're ready. People with no email on the **Staff roster** are listed in the Notify box.
