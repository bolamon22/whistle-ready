Staff training videos play inside each person's Whistle Ready login, and the app keeps track of who has watched them.

**To send videos to one person:** open their **Profile** on the **Staff Pool** or on a tournament's **Staff roster**. Under **Training videos** you see which of their videos they've watched (green, with a check) and when they were last sent videos. Click **Send the N not watched**, or **Send them again** once they've watched them all. Only that person gets the email.

**To send to a group:**
1. Open **People** → **Training videos**, tick the videos, and click **Send**.
2. To send to people working one event, pick it under **Working an event**. Roles then count only people on that event's staff roster. Pick **Everyone working it** to send to all of them.
3. Pick roles, such as **Scorekeepers** or **Athletic trainers**. A role that's on shows a check. Tap it again to turn it off.
4. Add people by name under **…and/or these people**, or type email addresses.
5. Under **Who gets it**, untick anyone who shouldn't get it. To send to only a few, click **Untick all**, then tick the ones you want.
6. Check the email on the right, then click **Send to N**.

**To see who has watched:** the **Training** column on the **Staff Pool** and on each **Staff roster** shows how many of the videos for their job they've watched, for example **3/11**. The **Who's watched** tab on **Training videos** has every person and video.

**Common problems:**
- Picking an event alone sends to nobody. Pick **Everyone working it**, a role or a person too.
- Someone with no email on file can't be sent videos. Add their email with **Edit**.
- Assigners send to the staff groups they run. Field directors send to the scorekeepers, athletic trainers and field ops on their events.
