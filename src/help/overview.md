Whistle Ready runs a tournament from setup to the final whistle. Each tournament has its own menu across the top.

**Where:** open a tournament from the **Tournaments** list, then use the top menu.

**The top menu:**
- **Dashboard** — the tournament at a glance: teams, games, staff and money.
- **Setup** — **Tournament setup**, **Divisions & teams**, **Scheduler**, **Assigner**, **Checklist** and **Documents**.
- **People** — **Team registrations**, **Player waivers**, **Coach waivers**, **Travel & hotels**, **Staff roster**, **Staff applications**, **Media credentials**, **Photo bookings**, **Photos & video sent in** and **Vendor requests**.
- **Live** — **Post scores**, **Assignments**, **Communications** and **Chirp insights**.
- **Financials** — revenue, expenses and profit.

You only see the menu items your role can open.

**The usual order of work:**
1. Fill in **Setup → Tournament setup** (dates, divisions, venues and fields, team fees, game timing), then click **Save Changes**.
2. As teams register, sort them into pools on **Setup → Divisions & teams** and generate pool games and brackets.
3. Place games on fields and times in **Setup → Scheduler**, then click **Publish** so the public page shows the schedule.
4. Put officials and scorekeepers on games in **Setup → Assigner**.
5. On game day, use **Live → Post scores** and **Live → Communications**.

The **Register** and **Public** buttons in the tournament header open the public registration form and the public schedule page.
