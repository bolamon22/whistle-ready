After you submit the team registration form you see a confirmation screen and get a confirmation email at the club contact email you entered.

**Where:** the screen appears right after you submit, or after you finish paying online. The email goes to the **Club Contact Email** on the form.

1. The screen title tells you where you stand: **Registration received!**, **Payment complete!**, or **You're on the waiting list** if every team you entered is in a full division.
2. It lists your club, each team with its division, and how many teams you entered. A team in a full division is marked **Waiting list**.
3. The box under your teams is your club portal, where you manage your roster and player waivers, track your balance and see your schedule once it is posted. What it says depends on your login (below).
4. Use **Event page** or **Game day** to get back to the event.

**The club portal box:**
- **Your club portal is ready** (a new login) or **Added to your club portal** (a login you already had): you're signed in. Tap **Open my club portal**. Next time, sign in with your contact email and your password.
- **That email already has a login**: the password you typed doesn't match that login, so nothing on it was changed. Your registration still went in. Tap **Add it to my portal** and enter your existing password. **Forgot your password?** opens the reset page in a new tab.
- **Sign in to open my portal**: you were already signed in, and your account just became a club director account. Tap it and sign in once more.
- **Set up your team account**: the registration went in without a portal password (for example, the tournament office entered it). Tap **Set up my account** to choose one.

The confirmation email repeats your teams and divisions, the amount due or paid, links to the **Event page**, the **Player waiver form** and **Game day**, and one portal button: **Open my club portal** if you chose a password on the form, **Sign in to my account** if the registration is already on your login, **Add it to my portal** if your email has a login the registration isn't on yet, or **Set up my account** otherwise. The organizer may change the email's wording.

**Common problems:**
- Teams on the waiting list are not charged. If a spot opens, you will be emailed.
- If you paid by bank transfer (ACH), it usually takes about 4 business days to clear, and the registration is marked paid when it does. Sometimes Stripe first sends a small deposit to verify your bank account; follow the emailed instructions to finish.
- If you chose Zelle or check, the form showed where to send payment. Your registration is saved either way.
- Questions about your registration: reply to the confirmation email.
