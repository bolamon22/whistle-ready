After you submit the team registration form you see a confirmation screen and get a confirmation email at the club contact email you entered.

**Where:** the screen appears right after you submit, or after you finish paying online. The email goes to the **Club Contact Email** on the form.

1. The screen title tells you where you stand: **Registration received!**, **Payment complete!**, or **You're on the waiting list** if every team you entered is in a full division.
2. It lists your club, each team with its division, and how many teams you entered. A team in a full division is marked **Waiting list**.
3. Tap **Set up my account** under **Set up your team account** to manage your roster and player waivers, track your balance and see your schedule once it is posted.
4. Use **Event page** or **Game day** to get back to the event.

The confirmation email repeats your teams and divisions, the amount due or paid, links to the **Event page**, the **Player waiver form** and **Game day**, and a **Set up my account** button (or **Sign in to my account** if you already have an account). The organizer may change the email's wording.

**Common problems:**
- Teams on the waiting list are not charged. If a spot opens, you will be emailed.
- If you paid by bank transfer (ACH), it usually takes about 4 business days to clear, and the registration is marked paid when it does. Sometimes Stripe first sends a small deposit to verify your bank account; follow the emailed instructions to finish.
- If you chose Zelle or check, the form showed where to send payment. Your registration is saved either way.
- Questions about your registration: reply to the confirmation email.
