Before you can schedule posts, connect your Instagram and Facebook accounts. You can also set queue times and see how posts performed.

**Where:** top bar → **Social** → the **Accounts & queue times** button.

**To connect accounts:**
1. Click **Connect Instagram + Facebook** (or **Add another account**).
2. Click **Continue with Meta** and sign in as the Page admin. Every Page you manage, and the Instagram account linked to it, gets connected.

**To import past posts:** click **Import post history**. It pulls the last 60 posts from each account with reach and likes. It is safe to run again; it only adds what is new.

**To set queue times:** under **Queue times**, set the times you like to post. **Add to queue** on a new post drops it into the next open time. Times are in your local time zone.

**To disconnect an account:** click **Disconnect**, then **Confirm**. Its scheduled posts move back to needs approval.

**To see results:** open the **Insights** tab for **By account**, **By format**, **When it lands** and **Posts ranked**. Insights update every 4 hours.

**Common problems:**
- Only directors and admins can manage accounts.
