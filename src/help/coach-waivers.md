Coach waivers shows which coaches signed the coach waiver, and lets you check them in at the coaches' tent.

**Where:** People → Coach waivers

**What you see:** the count signed and checked in, then each coach with role, certifications, club, teams, email, phone and the date they signed.

**To find a coach:** search by name, club or email, or pick a team from **All teams**.

**To check a coach in:** click **Check in** on their row. It changes to "In" with the time; click it again to undo.

**To check in a scanned credential:** scanning a coach's credential QR opens this page with them under **Scanned credential**. Click **Check in** (or **Undo**), then **Close**.

**To clear check-ins between days:** click **Clear check-ins** and confirm. It clears the selected team, or everyone. Waivers are untouched.

**To archive a coach** (duplicate or test): click the × on their row. Click **Archived** to see them and **Restore** to bring one back.

**Other tools:** **Open form** opens the coach waiver form to share. **CSV** downloads the list. The link icon opens a coach's credential.

**Common problems:**
- On Team registrations, a team showing "No coach waiver" has no signed coach waiver yet, even if a coach name was typed at registration.
