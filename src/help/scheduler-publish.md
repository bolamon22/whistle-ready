Moving games on the Scheduler is private. The public page shows times and fields as of your last **Publish**.

**Where:** **Setup → Scheduler**, top right.

**The status next to Weather:**
- **Draft** — never published.
- **Published** — the public page matches what you see.
- A number of **changes** — you have moved games since the last publish. Click it to see what changed.

**To publish:**
1. Click **Publish**.
2. If phones are following affected teams, a window lists games **Newly scheduled**, **Moved** and **Unscheduled**.
3. Leave **Tell followers their schedule changed** (or **Tell followers the schedule is out**) ticked to alert them, or untick it to publish quietly.
4. Click **Publish schedule**.

Followers get one message per team with its current schedule, never one per game. If nobody is following, it publishes right away.

**To choose what the public sees:**
1. Click the **Public:** pill (**Public: schedule**, **Public: pools only** or **Public: hidden**).
2. Switch **Teams & pools** (pool lists and standings) and **Schedule & brackets** on or off.

The same switches are on the **Public page** card on **Divisions & teams**.

**Common problems:**
- Turning on **Schedule & brackets** for the first time publishes the current schedule.
- Scores always update live, published or not.
- **Publish** is greyed out when there is nothing to publish.
