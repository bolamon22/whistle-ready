Businesses can apply for a booth at our events, or ask about sponsoring. Applying is free; you only pay after you're approved.

**Where:** our website's vendor page, at /register/vendor. The link in the "Our partners" section of the home page goes to its sponsorship part.

1. Under **Which weekends?**, pick every event you want. Each one is its own booth, reviewed and paid for separately.
2. Under **What a spot costs**, choose a booth type. Types marked **Not open** aren't taking applications.
3. Fill in the **Vendor application**: **Company name**, **Company contact**, **Mobile phone**, **Contact email**, and your website or Instagram.
4. Describe what you plan to sell (or what you'll be showcasing). Be specific; anything not listed has to stay in the truck.
5. Read the terms, tick **I have read and agree to the vendor terms above**, and tap **Submit application**.

How it works: you apply, we review, you're approved (with booth location, load-in and load-out times), then you pay by card or bank transfer to hold your space. No payment is taken now.

**Sponsoring instead?** Under **Looking to sponsor, not sell?**, tap **Request the sponsorship deck**, fill in your company and email, and tap **Send it over**.

**Common problems:**
- "Pick at least one event" or "Please choose a booth type" means a choice is missing.
- "That category is not accepting applications for this event" means that booth type is closed for the weekend you picked.
