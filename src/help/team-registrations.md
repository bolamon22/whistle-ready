The Team registrations page lists every club that registered, its teams, what it was invoiced, what it paid and what it still owes.

**Where:** People → Team registrations

**To find a club:**
1. Use **Search club, team, coach...**, **All Divisions** and **All Payment Status** (**Paid in Full**, **Partial Payment**, **No Payment**).
2. Click a club to expand it and see its teams, coaches, waiver counts and payments. **Clear filters** resets the list.

**To add a registration by hand:**
1. Click **Add entry**.
2. Fill in **Club Contact**, **Email** and **Phone** (required), plus **Club Name**, **Based In**, **Hotel?** and **Payment Method**.
3. Under **Teams**, fill in each team. Click **+ Add Team** for more.
4. Check the **Invoice** box. **Invoice Amount** is auto-calculated from your fees; type over it to override, and add a **Discount** and **Discount Reason** if needed.
5. Click **Save Registration**.

**To change a registration:** click **Edit**, make changes, then **Save Changes**.

**To delete or restore one:**
1. Click **Del** and confirm. The club moves to **Recently Deleted** at the bottom of the page.
2. Open **Recently Deleted** and click **Restore** to bring it back.

**Other tools:** **CSV** downloads the list, **Public form** opens your registration page, and **Returning teams** helps invite last year's clubs back. The chip under each contact shows **Account** if they have a Whistle Ready login or **No account** if not. For a club director, click **Account** to open their club portal exactly as they see it. Every button there opens its form so you can try it, but the last step (send, register, confirm) is left to the club.

If individual player registration is turned on, an **Individual Players** tab lists those players, with **Add entry**, **Edit** and **Del** for each.

**Common problems:**
- Saving a new entry can email the club its confirmation letter if **Email a copy to the club contact** is on in Team fees.
- Deleting a registration takes its teams' games off the schedule. Restoring brings the teams back, not the games.
- Deleted entries are kept 30 days; anything with payments is kept permanently.
- "Couldn't load your registrations" is a loading problem, not lost data. Click **Try again**.
