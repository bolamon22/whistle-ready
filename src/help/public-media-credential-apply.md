Photographers and videographers who want to shoot our events apply for a media credential first.

**Where:** on the **Gallery** page, tap **Shoot with us**. On the next page, tap **Apply for a credential**.

1. Fill in **Your name**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Portfolio link** (your portfolio is what we judge you on). **Business name** is optional.
2. Under **Where do you post?**, tick every platform you use, and add your handle.
3. Under **Which events are you shooting?**, pick at least one event.
4. Under **What do you want to do?**, choose how far you want to take it (each option explains what it includes).
5. Optionally tell us **Stills or video?**, your **Camera and lenses**, whether you have **Liability insurance?**, and **Anything else**.
6. Read the terms, including **Photos of minors.**, and tick **I have read and agree to the above**.
7. Tap **Send my application**.

A preview of your credential builds as you type. It is marked not valid yet until you're approved. The organizer reviews every application. If you're approved, you get an email with a private link to your credential page (see "Use your media credential link").

**Common problems:**
- "Pick at least one event." or "Tell us what you want to do." means a required choice is missing.
- "Please confirm you have read the terms." Tick the agreement box.
