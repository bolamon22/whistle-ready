The staff directory lists everyone on this tournament's staff roster, grouped by job, with buttons to call or text them.

**Where:** **Live → Communications**, **Contacts** tab, or **Staff contacts** on your home page.

**To find someone:**
1. Type in **Search staff…**, or scroll the groups: **Field Ops**, **Athletic Trainers**, **Scorekeepers**, **Assigners**, **Referees**, **Directors** and **Staff**.
2. Tap the phone button to call or the message button to text.

**Common problems:**
- **no #** means there is no phone number on file. An organizer can add it in the Staff Pool.
- Only people on the tournament's **Staff roster** appear here.
