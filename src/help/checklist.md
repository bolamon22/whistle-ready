The setup checklist is one shared list for the whole event. Anyone on staff can check items off and add their own, and everyone sees the same list.

**Where:** **Setup → Checklist**, or **Setup checklist** on a field ops worker's home page.

**To use it:**
1. Tap an item to check it off. It shows who checked it and when. Tap again to uncheck it.
2. To add an item, type in **Add a setup item…** and tap **Add**.
3. To remove an item, point at it and click the trash icon.

The top shows how many items are done and a progress bar. Changes save automatically and are shared with everyone on staff.

**Common problems:**
- The trash icon only appears when you hover with a mouse, so items are hard to remove from a phone.
