Travel & hotels totals how many clubs are staying overnight and how many room nights they booked. These totals feed sports-tourism grant applications and post-event reports.

**Where:** People → Travel & hotels

**What you see:**
- Totals for **Clubs registered**, **Clubs staying overnight**, **Rooms / night (est.)** and **Total room nights**.
- One row per club with its teams, **Based in**, **Hotel?** answer from registration, its hotels (rooms x nights) and room nights.
- **Room nights by hotel**, which matches the grant post-event report table.

**To change a club's hotel answer:** pick **Yes**, **No** or **Maybe** under **Hotel?**. It saves when you click away.

**To add hotel bookings:** hotels, rooms and nights are logged per booking on the housing board. Click **Housing company** to open it. A club can split across several hotels.

**To print the report:** click **Print**.

**Common problems:**
- "TBD" means no booking has been logged for that club yet.
- Bookings without a hotel name are totaled as "Hotel TBD".
- Hotels must send room-block confirmation letters (or a booking-agent report) with the post-event report.
