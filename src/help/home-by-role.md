After you sign in, Whistle Ready opens a home page that fits your role. Each one lists your tournaments with shortcut buttons, plus a globe icon for the public view.

**Director home:**
- Shows **Hi, (your name)** and a **Your team** button for adding staff.
- Each tournament has **Manage**, **Scores**, **Field Req** and **Broadcast**.
- With no tournaments yet, it shows two starting steps: **Add your first tournament** and **Add your staff & admins**.
- Clicking **Tournaments** in the top bar opens the full tournaments list.

**Assigner home:**
- Each tournament has **Schedule**, **Assign**, **Avail** and **Field Req**.

**Scheduler home:**
- The next event shows under **Up next**.
- Each tournament has **Schedule**, **Divisions**, **Manage** and **Setup**, and a progress bar such as "198 games · 11 placed". "No games yet" means no schedule exists yet.
- Use **Find a tournament** to search, and the upcoming, past and all buttons to filter.

**Admins** land on the tournaments list, with the **Admin** menu for org tools.

**Common problems:**
- If you see a page you did not expect, your role may be different from what you think. Ask an admin to check it in **Users**.
