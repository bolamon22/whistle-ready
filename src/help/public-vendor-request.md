Businesses can apply for a vendor booth at an event. The organizer reviews every application first; nothing is charged until you are approved.

**Where:** **Vendor Request** in the event page's **Event info** menu, or /tournaments/<id>/vendor-request.

1. If more than one event is listed under **Which weekends?**, tick each one you want. Each is its own booth, reviewed and paid separately.
2. Under **What a spot costs**, pick a booth type. Each shows its fee, **Fee on approval**, or **Not open** if that type is not taking applications.
3. Under **Vendor application**, fill in **Company name**, **Company contact**, **Mobile phone** and **Contact email**. **Website or Instagram** is optional.
4. Describe what you will sell (or showcase). Be specific.
5. Read the vendor terms and tick **I have read and agree to the vendor terms above**.
6. Tap **Submit application** (the button may show your booth and total instead).

You will see a confirmation (usually **Application received**) with a summary of your application.

How it works: you apply, the organizer reviews (you hear back either way), approved vendors get booth location and load-in details, then a link to pay by card or bank transfer. Paying holds your space.

**Common problems:**
- No payment is taken when you apply.
- **We aren't accepting vendor applications for this event right now.** means every booth type is closed.
- Want to sponsor instead of sell? Under **Looking to sponsor, not sell?** tap **Request the sponsorship deck**, fill in **Company** and **Email**, and tap **Send it over**.
