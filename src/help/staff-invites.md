Staff need their own login to see their games, open the scorekeeper and use the game day tools. You can email invites, copy a personal link to text, or share one public signup link with new recruits.

**Where:** **Staff** in the top bar. A signup link is also on **People → Staff roster**.

**To invite people already in your pool:**
1. On the Staff Pool, find the person. The **App** column shows **Registered**, **Invited**, **Not invited** or **No email**.
2. Click **Invite** (or **Invite to app** on a phone). If they were invited before, the button says **Resend**. This emails them their own login link.
3. To invite several at once, tick them and click **Send app invites**.

**To text someone a link instead:** click **Copy link** next to their name, then paste it into a text. Use this for anyone marked **No email**.

**To recruit new people:** click **Recruiting link**. It copies your organization's public staff signup link; paste it into your own email or text. On a tournament's **Staff roster**, **Copy signup link** copies the same link.

**To edit and send the invite letter:**
1. Click **Invite letter**.
2. Pick who it is for: **Existing staff — in your pool already**, **New recruits — not in the pool yet**, or **Assigner — to forward to their officials**.
3. Edit the **Subject** and **Letter**. **Reset to default** puts back the standard wording.
4. Under **Send it now**, type up to 10 email addresses, separated by commas, and click **Save + send**. Existing staff each get their own login link; new recruits get your signup link.

**To invite one person to a single tournament:** on **People → Staff roster**, click **Invite Staff**, enter **Email address** and an optional **Name**, and click **Send Invite**. They receive an email to set up their profile.

**Common problems:**
- Every invite option above sends a real email to the staff member.
- **Invite** is hidden for people with no email on file. Use **Copy link** for them.
