Organizer answers about paying for your teams.

**Do we pay when we register?**
You don't have to pay to register. Full payment before the event confirms your spots, and we can't take payment at the fields.

**How can we pay?**
- **Online portal:** bank transfer (ACH) is free; credit or debit card, or Apple Pay, has a 3% processing fee.
- **Zelle:** send to info@sunshinelax.com.
- **Check:** payable to Sunshine Events Group, mailed to 11830 Wiles Rd, Coral Springs, FL 33076.

**We paid but it still shows a balance.**
Log out and log back in to refresh your account. Don't submit another payment. If the balance still shows after logging back in, email info@sunshinelax.com.
