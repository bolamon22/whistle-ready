Some events let a single player register without a team. This form collects the player's details, sizes and a signed waiver, then takes payment by card.

**Where:** a link the organizer shares for individual registration. The older links ending in individual-register or player-register open the same form. If the event does not offer it, you will see **Registration not open**.

1. Under **Player Information**, fill in **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Date of Birth** and **USA Lacrosse member #**. **Phone** is optional. Tap **Look up my member number** if you don't know it.
2. For players under 18, fill in **Parent / Guardian**: **Guardian Name** and **Guardian Phone** (**Guardian Email** is optional).
3. Under **Emergency Contact**, fill in **Name**, **Phone** and **Relationship**. **Medical Notes** is optional.
4. Under **Playing Details**, tap a **Position**, a **Jersey Size** and a **Shorts Size**. **Jersey Number Request** is optional.
5. Under **Registration Fee**, pick an option. The form shows the subtotal, processing fees and **Amount Due**.
6. Read the **Waiver & Release**, tick **I have read and agree to the waiver and release of liability above**, and type your full name in **Type your full name as signature**.
7. Tap **Continue to Payment**, check the **Order Summary**, enter your **Card Details** and tap **Pay**.

When payment goes through you will see **You're registered!** Tap **View Tournament** to see the schedule.

**Common problems:**
- This form takes card payment only.
- Position, jersey size and shorts size must be picked before you can continue.
- Tap **Edit registration** on the payment step to go back and fix something.
- This is different from the player waiver that players on a registered team fill out.
