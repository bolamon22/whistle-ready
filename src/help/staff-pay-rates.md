**Staff pay rates** sets each staff role's pay and how many officials each game needs. The Assigner and the pay report use these numbers.

**Where:** **Setup → Tournament setup → Staff pay rates**, or **Pay rates** at the top of **Staff pay** (**People → Staff pay**). On the Whistle Ready Assigner plan, which has no Tournament setup, **Pay rates** is where the Head Assigner sets them. There, click **Save pay rates** instead of **Save Changes**.

**To set roles and rates:**
1. Under **Staff Roles & Pay Rates**, enter a **Rate** for each **Role** and pick **Per game** or **Per hour**.
2. To add a role, type it in **New role name…** and click the add button. The remove button deletes a role.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

**Load my defaults** fills in your saved roles and rates. **Save as global defaults** saves the current ones for future tournaments (you may also be asked **Save these roles and rates as your default for future tournaments?** — choose **Yes, save globally** or **Dismiss**).

**To set how many officials a game needs:**
1. Under **Officials Per Game**, set the **Title for officials at this tournament** (for example Official, Referee or Umpire). It is used on schedules, assignments and notifications.
2. Pick the **Standard officials per game**.
3. For divisions that need a different number, click **Add Exception**, type text the division name contains (for example "7v7" or "U8") and pick **1 official**, **2 officials** or **3 officials**.
4. Tick **Championship games use a different number of officials** and set **Officials per championship game** if finals need more.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

**Common problems:**
- With no exceptions, every game uses the standard count.
- A new rate applies to games assigned after you save. Games already assigned keep the pay they were given.
- A **Pay Rate Override** on someone's Staff Pool card comes first, and time entries are paid at the **Hourly Rate** on each person's card.
- A role you add is saved, but pay comes from the roles every event starts with.
