**Financials** shows the tournament's profit and loss: registration fees, vendor booths, staff pay and any other income or expenses you add.

**Where:** **Financials** in the tournament menu.

**What is on it:**
- Top tiles: **Total Revenue**, **Total Expenses**, **Gross Profit** and **Net Cash**.
- **P&L Summary**: **Revenue** (registration fees, approved vendor booths, other income), **Collected so far**, **Outstanding from teams**, **Outstanding from vendors**, then **Expenses** (staff pay, other expenses), **Paid out to staff**, **Staff still owed**, **Net Cash Position** and **Expenses as % of revenue**.
- Shortcuts: **Team Fees** (Team registrations), **Vendors** (Vendor requests) and **Staff Pay** (the Pay Summary).

**To add an expense or income:**
1. Click **Add Expense** or **Add Income**.
2. Pick the **Category** (for example **Facility / Fields**, **Rentals**, **Awards & Trophies**, **Sponsorship**, **Gate / Admission**).
3. Enter a **Description** and **Amount** (both required), plus **Payment Method**, **Date** and **Notes** if you like.
4. Click **Add Expense** / **Add Income**.

**To see, edit or delete them:** open the **Other** tab. Use **Edit** or **Delete** on a row.

**Team payments and refunds** are not entered here. Record a payment or refund a payment on **People → Team registrations**; Financials picks it up.

**Common problems:**
- Deleting a transaction asks for confirmation.
- Staff costs come from assignments and pay rates; mark staff paid on the Pay Summary.
