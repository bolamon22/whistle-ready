**Financials** shows the tournament's money: what was billed and collected, what it costs, and the profit.

**Where:** **Financials** in the tournament menu.

**The tabs:**
- **Overview**: **Profit when everything is collected and paid** (billed minus everything committed) and **Cash in hand today** (collected minus paid out), then **Still to collect**, **Still to pay** and **Bills waiting to come in**. Under them, **Income** (team fees, players, vendor booths, other income) and **Bills & expenses** (vendor bills, **Referees & game staff**, **Receipts & purchases** and anything typed on **Other entries**). Tap the arrow on a row for its detail.
- **Budget**: the projected profit, with a planned amount for each income and cost line.
- **Expenses**: receipts for things someone bought for the event. See "Receipts: approve them and pay people back".
- **Other entries**: income and expenses you type in by hand.
- Shortcuts: **Team Fees** (Team registrations), **Vendors** (Vendor requests) and **Staff pay**.

**To add an expense or income by hand:**
1. On **Other entries**, click **Add Expense** or **Add Income** (**Add income** on **Overview** opens the same form).
2. Pick the **Category** (for example **Facility / Fields**, **Rentals**, **Awards & Trophies**, **Sponsorship**, **Gate / Admission**).
3. Enter a **Description** and **Amount** (both required), plus **Payment Method**, **Date** and **Notes** if you like.
4. Click **Add Expense** / **Add Income**.

To change or remove one, use **Edit** or **Delete** on its row. Receipts someone turns in go on **Expenses**, not here.

**Team payments and refunds** are not entered here. Record a payment or refund a payment on **People → Team registrations**; Financials picks it up.

**Common problems:**
- Deleting a transaction asks for confirmation.
- Staff costs come from assignments and pay rates; mark staff paid on **Staff pay**.
