At events that use player cards, each player gets a card after the waiver is signed. It shows the player's name, team, photo and a QR code.

**Where:** the card appears on the confirmation screen right after you submit the player waiver (**Save**, **Print**, **Open**). The link to the card page is also in your confirmation email.

1. Open the card page. It shows **Player card** with the player's name and team.
2. Tap **Save to phone** to download the card. On iPhone you can also press and hold the card to add it to Photos.
3. Tap **Print card** to print it. It prints at badge size, so you can cut it out and put it in a standard lanyard holder.
4. Tap **Add photo** (or **Change photo**) to put a photo on the card. **Remove photo** takes it off.
5. Tap **Share** to send the card link. On some phones it shows **Link copied**.
6. Under **QR code opens**, paste a link (highlight reel, Instagram, Hudl) and tap **Save**. Leave it blank and the code opens the card page.

Below the card you can see the **Tournament**, **Dates**, **Player ID** and **Waiver on file** date. Tap **Register another player** to fill out a waiver for a sibling or teammate.

**Common problems:**
- The card is not the waiver. The signed waiver is what counts.
- Anyone with the card link can change the photo and the QR link, so share it only with family.
- Not every event uses player cards. If you didn't get one, the waiver is still on file.
