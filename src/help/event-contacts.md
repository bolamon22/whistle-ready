**Event contacts** is your directory of everyone you work with to put on an event: the city and its fields, the county, the sports commission, safety and medical, rentals, food and vendors, officials, insurance and housing. Each contact shows what you get from them, what they need from you, and who owes the next reply.

**Where:** **Contacts** in the top bar (on a phone, **Event contacts** in the menu). A tournament's own contacts are on its **Contacts** tab.

**To add a contact:**
1. Click **Add contact**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Title**, **Organization**, **Category**, **Phone**, **Email** and **Address**.
3. Under **Events**, tick the events they work on, or **Every event**. With no event ticked, they show in the directory only.
4. Fill in **What we get from them**, **What they need from us**, **Notes** and **Last email or call**, then click **Add contact**.

**To find someone:** type in **Search name, org, email, phone…**, or tap an event (**All events**, one event, or **Every event**) or a category. The boxes along the top filter too: **we owe a reply**, **no reply in 2+ weeks** and **open items linked**.

**To work with a contact:** click them to open their details.
- Set **Who owes a reply**: **We owe a reply**, **Waiting on them** or **Nothing pending**. Click **Talked today** after a call or email.
- **Open items** are the event tasks linked to them. Link a task to a contact from the task's details on the **Tasks** page.
- **Write an email** opens a ready-made or AI-written email to them (see **Keep track of tasks for each event**).
- **Send a document** emails a PDF, like a certificate of insurance, to the contacts you pick (and any other address), with a ready-made message you can change. **Email it to me to forward** sends it to you instead.
- The pencil edits the contact; **Delete** removes it after you confirm.

**On a tournament's Contacts tab:** the contacts tagged with that event. **Add from the directory** adds someone already in the directory, **New** adds a new contact, and **Open the directory** goes to the full list. The event's tasks show alongside, with **Open in Tasks**.

**Import:** **Import** loads a contacts file (.json), like the one made from a scan of your email. Contacts already in the directory are skipped.

**Common problems:**
- Only directors and admins have **Event contacts**.
- A contact you can't find under an event: check they have that event ticked, or **Every event**.
