The **Club database** lists every club that has registered for your events, with each club's history of events, teams and divisions. Use it to find returning clubs and invite back the ones who skipped.

**Where:** Home → **Admin** → **Club database**.

**To build or update it:**
1. Click **Import registration exports** (or **Import more** once you have data).
2. Pick one or more .xlsx registration spreadsheets. Each needs a Club Name column.
3. Imports add up. Re-importing a file refreshes that event's numbers instead of duplicating them.

**To find clubs:**
1. Type in **Search club, contact, city…**.
2. Pick a tournament from **All tournaments** to narrow the list.
3. Click **All**, **Returning** (came two or more years) or **Win-back**.

**To work a club:** click its name to expand it. You can:
- Click **Draft win-back email**. This opens your own email app with a prefilled invite. Nothing is sent until you send it.
- Call the phone number or open the **Website**.
- Add a **Note** (for example, folded or renamed). It saves when you click away and survives re-imports.
- Click **Archive** to hide a club. See them again under **Archived**, and use **Unarchive** to bring one back.

**Common problems:**
- With no tournament picked, **Win-back** means the club came to an event in 2024 but not in 2025. With a tournament picked, it means the club came before but missed that tournament's most recent year.
- **Clear** wipes the whole database after a confirmation. You can only rebuild it by importing again.
- A file without a Club Name column is skipped.
