Photographers credentialed by the organizer list their packages on our website. You send a request, and the photographer contacts you directly to confirm price and details.

**Where:** on the **Gallery** page, tap **Book photo & video**, or tap **Book** on a photographer's card under it.

1. Open **Photo & video** to see every photographer. Tap **Portfolio** to see their work, or **Book** to open their page.
2. On their page, check **Packages**, then go to **Request a shoot**.
3. Pick the **Event** and the **Package**.
4. Fill in **Your name**, **Phone**, **Email** and **Player name**.
5. For **Club / team**, start typing and pick your team from the list. Then fill in **Division**, and **Jersey number** if you know it.
6. Add anything else in **Anything else** (a sibling on another team, a game you want covered).
7. Tap the send button at the bottom. You'll see **Request sent**.

Nothing is charged on this page. Pricing, delivery and payment are between you and the photographer; the organizer does not employ them.

**Common problems:**
- "No events open right now" in the **Event** list means this photographer isn't booked for an upcoming event.
- "No photographers listed yet." means none are taking bookings right now.
