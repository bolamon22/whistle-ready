The Field board shows every game on every field with its refs and scorekeeper by name. Nobody can change anything here, so it's safe to keep open at the fields. It's for field directors, tournament directors, assigners and schedulers.

**Where:** **Field board** on the field director home page, **Live → Field board** on the event dashboard, or **Live → Field board** in the event menu.

**To read it:**
1. Pick the day at the top. Today opens first on game day.
2. On a computer, fields run across and times run down. Each time shows how many of its games have everyone they need.
3. Red means a spot nobody has yet: **Need ref** or **Need scorekeeper**.
4. Tap a name to call that person. Field directors see phone numbers for their own staff only.

**To see just refs or just scorekeepers:**
1. Tap **Refs** or **Scorekeepers**. Each game folds down to only those people, and the count of open spots counts only them.
2. Tap a game to see its teams, or tap **Show teams** to open every game.
3. Tap **Everyone** to go back.

**Other filters:**
- **Boys** or **Girls** shows only those games.
- Tap the red **open spots** button to show only the games still missing someone.

**On a phone:** pick a time along the top. Each field is a card. Times with an open spot are outlined in red.

**To print it:** set the day and filters you want (**Refs** or **Scorekeepers**, **Boys** or **Girls**, open spots only), then click **Print**. It prints the table sideways (landscape) with the event, the day and the filters at the top, on a phone too. To print every day at once, or by field, click **All days** (see "Print the schedule and see who had games before they moved").

The page refreshes every minute. To move or add someone, use the Assigner.

**Common problems:**
- A game is missing: it needs a day, a time and a field on the schedule first. Canceled games don't show.
- No phone number on a name: a field director only gets numbers for the staff they run, not refs.
