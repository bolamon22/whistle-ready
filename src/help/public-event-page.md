The event page is the home page for a tournament: dates, location, divisions, team fees, hotels, contacts and links to every form.

**Where:** /tournaments/<id>/event, or the link from the organizer's website or emails.

1. The top shows the event name, dates and location, and sometimes a status badge (for example **Full**, **Closing soon** or **Final results**).
2. **Register a team** opens team registration. It only shows while registration is open.
3. Tap **Event info** to jump to a section: **Overview**, **Tournament fees**, **Divisions**, **Location**, **Hotels**, **Rules**, **Contacts**, **Sponsors & partners**, plus **Coach waiver**, **Game day hub**, **Vendor Request** and **Work at our event**. Only sections the organizer filled in appear.
4. The strip under the title has shortcuts: **DATES**, **LOCATION**, **DIVISIONS**, **TEAM FEE**, **HOTELS**, **WAIVER** (**Player waiver**) and **SCHEDULE** (**View games**). Swipe it sideways on a phone.
5. **Team fees** shows the fee for this event. **Divisions** lists every division; some show a note like **Full**. **Location** has **Get directions**. **Hotels** has **Book hotels**.
6. **For coaches** links to **Coach waiver & credential**. **For players & parents** links to **Player waiver**, **Rules & policies**, **Schedule & live scores** and **Game day hub**.
7. **Questions?** lists the event contacts.

**Common problems:**
- Fees and divisions differ by event. The event page and registration form show the current ones.
- Spots are confirmed once registration and payment are complete.
- After the event ends, the **Register a team** button goes away.
