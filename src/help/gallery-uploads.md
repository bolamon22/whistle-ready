Photos and video sent in by credentialed photographers and creators wait here for review. Nothing reaches the public gallery until you publish it.

**Where:** People → Photos & video sent in

**The tabs:**
- **Needs review**: new files waiting on you.
- **Live**: published to the public gallery.
- **Not used**: files you set aside.

**To publish photos:**
1. On **Needs review**, click photos to select them, or click **Select all**.
2. Click **Publish**.

**To set photos aside:** select them and click **Don't use**.

**To take a published photo down:** on **Live**, select it and click **Take down**.

**To delete files:** select them, click **Delete** and confirm.

Click **Load more** at the bottom to see more files.

**Common problems:**
- **Delete** removes the files from the gallery for good.
- The buttons stay disabled until at least one file is selected.
