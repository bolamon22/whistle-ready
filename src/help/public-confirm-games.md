When your assigner releases the schedule, they may ask you to confirm the games you were given. This page lists your games and lets you confirm them in one tap, or tell the assigner which ones you can't work. No login is needed.

**Where:** the **Confirm my games** button in the "Please confirm your games" email, or **Confirm now** on the **Confirm your games** card on your staff home.

1. Open the link. Your games are listed by day, with time, field, game number, teams and your job. Each one starts as **✓ Can work**.
2. If you can work them all, tap **Confirm my N games**. You'll see **Thanks** and **You're confirmed for N games**.
3. Can't work one? Tap its **✓ Can work** button so it turns red and says **Can't work**, and add why if you like (your assigner sees it). Then tap the button at the bottom, which now says something like **Confirm 4 games, can't work 1**.
4. Check the message (**You'll come off game #205, and your assigner will be told**) and tap **Yes, send it**. That game comes off your schedule right away, and your assigner gets an email.

**Common problems:**
- **Less than 48 hours away: call or text the assigner.** A game can't be turned down here once its day is less than 48 hours off (Saturday from Thursday on).
- **Changed since you confirmed** means the game moved after you confirmed it. Confirm it again.
- Changed your mind about a game you turned down? It's off your schedule, so call or text the assigner.
- The link only works for your own games. If it says it isn't working, open My games in Whistle Ready or ask the assigner for a new email.
