When the same person ends up in the Staff Pool twice (for example imported as "Kathryn" and signed up as "Katie"), merge the two records into one so their roster spots, availability, assignments and pay history stay together.

**Where:** **Staff** in the top bar.

**To merge a suggested pair:**
1. If the app spots likely duplicates, a **possible duplicates** button appears above the list. Click it.
2. Under **Possible duplicates**, look at each pair.
3. Click **Keep this one** under the record you want to keep. The other record's history moves onto it and the duplicate is deleted.
4. If the two are really different people, click **These are different people** so the pair stops showing.

**To merge any two records yourself:**
1. Tick exactly two people in the list.
2. Click **Merge these two**.
3. Under **Merge these two into one person**, click **Keep this one** on the record to keep, then confirm.

**Common problems:**
- A merge can't be undone. The kept record also picks up any detail it was missing (email, phone, pay details, cert level) from the other one, so check both before you confirm.
- **Merge these two** only appears when exactly two people are ticked.
