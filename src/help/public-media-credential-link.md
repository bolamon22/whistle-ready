When the organizer approves your media application, you get an email with a private link. That page is your credential, and it's where you send photos and edit your booking page.

**Where:** the link in your approval email. Keep it; it's your pass for the weekend.

1. Open the link. The page shows your **Media credential** marked **Approved**, the event under **Good for**, and what **You're approved for**. Show this screen at check-in.
2. Read the event details: **Where to go**, **Checking in**, **Event times**, **Field rules**, **Sending us your photos** and **Who to call**. If you see **Details are still being finalised.**, check back on the same link.
3. To send photos, go to **Send us your photos**. Drop files in, or tap **Choose files**. JPEG or PNG photos and short MP4 clips, up to 60 files at a time. Each file shows its status, such as **Waiting on the organizer** or **Live in the gallery**.
4. If you have a booking page, tap **Edit page** under **Your booking page**. Update your name, bio, links and **Packages**, then tap **Save my page**.

**Common problems:**
- **Still under review** means a decision hasn't been made yet. "We couldn't fit you in this time" means you weren't credentialed for that event; you can apply again for the next one.
- More than 60 files: only the first 60 are taken. Send the rest in a second batch.
- "Your files uploaded but we could not record them." Tell the organizer before you re-send.
