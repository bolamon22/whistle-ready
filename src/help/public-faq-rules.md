Organizer answers about playing rules. The event's **Rules** page has the full rules and wins if anything here differs.

**What's the youngest age group?**
U8 is the youngest division, if enough teams sign up. U8 and U10 play 7v7 on a small field with goalies. There's no 6U.

**Is high school full field? Are long poles limited?**
High school plays full field (10v10 boys, 12v12 girls) under standard rules, including long-pole limits. The 7v7 divisions allow at most 2 long poles.

**How long are games? Is there a mercy rule?**
Games are two 20-minute running halves. Below high school, a 6-goal lead triggers the mercy rule. Each team gets one timeout per half.

**How are ties decided?**
Pool ties go to a 3v3 Braveheart. Championship games go to sudden-death overtime.

**Do lower-school girls check?**
No. There's no checking in lower-school girls.

**Do middle-school girls play modified or transitional checking?**
Middle-school girls follow USA Lacrosse U14 rules with transitional checking.
