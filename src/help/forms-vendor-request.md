The **Vendor request** form lets vendors apply for a booth. You approve them, then they pay.

**Where:** Home → **Admin** → **Forms** → **Vendor request** (admins). Directors can open [Forms](/dashboard/org/forms) directly.

**To edit the vendor form:**
1. Open **Vendor request** and click **Edit**. The public link is shown at the top with **View** and **Copy**.
2. Set the **Header photo** (**Upload** or **Remove**), **Headline** and **Sub-headline**.
3. Under **Booth types**, give each type a name, a fee, and one line the applicant sees. Tick **Sells product on site** or **Listed but closed** as needed. Use **+ Add a booth type** to add more, or the X to remove one.
4. In **Notify me at**, enter the email that should hear about each new application. To notify someone for certain events only, use **Add person** and tick their events.
5. Write the **Approval notice**, shown at the top of the form so nobody assumes submitting reserves a spot.
6. Fill in **Approved-vendor instructions**: **Where to set up**, **Event times**, **Load-in**, **Load-out**, **What to bring**, **Who to contact**.
7. Optional: tick **Offer web sponsorship as a booth add-on** and **Show the sponsorship section**, then fill in the levels with **+ Add a sponsorship level**.
8. Edit the **Vendor disclaimer**, **Confirmation title** and **Confirmation message**, and tick **Email a confirmation to the vendor** if wanted.
9. Click **Save**.

**Common problems:**
- A booth fee of 0 shows "confirmed on approval" on the form instead of a number.
- **Notify me at** left blank uses your org contact address.
- Any instruction left blank is simply left out. If all are blank, approved vendors get no setup details.
