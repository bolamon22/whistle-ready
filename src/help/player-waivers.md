Player waivers lists every waiver parents signed for this tournament. Use it to look players up, fix the team they picked, and see each team's roster.

**Where:** People → Player waivers

**To find players:**
1. Search by player, parent, email, phone or USA Lacrosse number.
2. Pick a team from **All teams** to see that team's roster. Sort by **Newest first**, **Name A–Z** or **Jersey #**.
3. Click a player to see every field they submitted.

**To fix a waiver:** open the player, click **Edit**, change the details (re-pick the team if it's wrong), then **Save changes**.

**To sort out waivers not on a registered team:**
1. The yellow panel "waivers not on a registered team" lists team names that don't match a registration.
2. Choose a team in **move all N to** and click **Move all N**, or click **Assign these N individually** and pick a team per player, then **Move**.

**To remove a player:** open them and click **Archive** (not attending, duplicate or test). Archived players are left out of counts, check-in and badges. Click **Archived (N)** to see them, then **Restore** or **Delete permanently**.

**Other tools:** **Open form** opens the waiver form to share. **Export CSV** downloads the list (or just the filtered players).

**Common problems:**
- A warning "Filed under club..." means the player's club doesn't match the team they picked, so they count against the wrong club. Edit and re-pick the team.
- **Delete permanently** asks you to tap again and can't be undone.
- Waiver counts on Team registrations link here, filtered to that team.
