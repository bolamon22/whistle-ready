Organizer answers about the schedule and tournament format.

**When is the schedule posted?**
The schedule is posted on the event page in the days before the tournament and can change until game day.

**Can our team start later because we're traveling?**
We can't move start times for one team, since a whole division plays in the same slots. Games can start at 8:00 AM, so traveling teams usually book Friday and Saturday nights.

**Can our games be closer together?**
Email info@sunshinelax.com and we'll look, but we can't promise changes once the schedule is set.

**What time does Sunday end?**
We can't give an exact time. Many championships finish by about 2 PM, depending on the division and the weather.

**Do we play both days?**
Yes. Teams play Saturday and Sunday.

**How many games are guaranteed?**
Every team is guaranteed at least 4 games. Formats vary with division size: some divisions play two pool games and then reseed, others play three pool games before reseeded playoff rounds.

**Do B divisions have playoffs?**
Yes. Every division has playoffs.
