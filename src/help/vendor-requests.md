Vendor requests lists businesses that applied for a booth. You approve them with a booth fee, and they get a link to their booth page to pay.

**Where:** People → Vendor requests

**What you see:** each request's **Company**, **Contact**, **Level**, **Status** and when it was **Submitted**. Status shows **Needs review**, **Approved**, **Declined** or **Paid**.

**To approve a vendor:**
1. Click the row to open it.
2. Click **Approve & send link**.
3. Confirm the booth fee in the box (leave it as-is to use their booth type's price) and click OK.
4. A message says whether the vendor was emailed their booth page.

**To decline:** click **Decline** and confirm. Nothing is charged.

**To undo an approval or decline:** click **Undo**.

**To resend the booth page:** click **Copy booth link** on an approved vendor and send it yourself.

**To delete a request:** click **Delete** and confirm.

**Other tools:** **Open form** opens the vendor application. **Export CSV** downloads the list with status, fee and payment (and the invoice number, paid and balance for directors).

**Invoices:** open an approved vendor's row to see its **Invoice** box (directors and admins). It shows the invoice number, the booth fee, what's been paid and what's due.
- **Open PDF** shows the invoice: the booth type, the web sponsorship on its own line if they added it, payments so far, and how to pay by check, Zelle or online.
- **Email invoice** sends the PDF to the vendor, and to any other address you add (their accounting office, say). A copy goes to the office inbox.
- **Record payment** is for a check, Zelle or cash: enter the amount, how they paid and the date. A vendor paid in full shows **Paid**; part of the way, **Part paid**. Payments on their booth page are recorded by themselves.
- To take off a payment entered by mistake, click the trash can next to it.
- Vendors can download the same invoice from their booth page.

**QuickBooks:** vendor invoices go to QuickBooks the same way club invoices do, with the next invoice number, when the QuickBooks sync is on (People → Team registrations → **QuickBooks**). A vendor approved after the sync was turned on goes by itself. One approved before then shows **Not in QuickBooks yet**: click **Send to QuickBooks**. **Email invoice** sends it to QuickBooks first, so the PDF carries the QuickBooks number. Payments follow every 15 minutes, or click **Sync now** in the **Invoice** box.

**Common problems:**
- **Approve & send link** emails the vendor right away.
- If the email didn't send, copy their booth link and send it yourself.
- **Decline** and **Undo** are not available once a vendor has paid anything. Take the payments off first.
- **Delete** is refused while the vendor's invoice is in QuickBooks. Decline the vendor instead and void the invoice in QuickBooks.
- Taking off a payment that's already in QuickBooks doesn't take it off there. Delete it in QuickBooks too.
- If QuickBooks can't be reached, **Email invoice** still sends, with Whistle Ready's number (SEG-V-…), and says why.
- **Delete** can't be undone.
