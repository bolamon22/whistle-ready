The **Photographers** page controls who appears on your Book photo & video page and what you ask of photographers in return for a credential.

**Where:** Home → **Admin** → **Photographers**.

**To set what you ask of photographers:**
1. Under **What we ask photographers for**, set **Minimum photos** and **Within (days)**.
2. Under **Booking tier asks for extra**, set **Posts before the event**, **Minimum photos**, **Within (hours)** and **Your Instagram**.
3. Tick **Tag us in their posts** or **Ask for a Collab invite on their posts**, and add **Anything else** (one per line).
4. Enter **Where credential applications land**. Blank uses your org contact email.
5. Click that box's **Save**. These appear on the Shoot with us page, the application and the confirmation email.

**To add a photographer:**
1. Click **Add**.
2. Fill in **Business name**, **Person**, **Booking email** (requests go here), **Location**, **Website** and **Instagram**.
3. Check the **URL**, add a **Bio**, a **Headshot / logo**, a **Cover photo** and up to 6 **Sample photos**.
4. Add **Packages** with **+ Add a package**. A price of 0 shows "Ask".
5. Under **Events they shoot**, tick events. None ticked means every event.
6. Tick **Live** to show them, then click **Save**.

Use the arrows to change the order, and **Preview page** to see their page.

**Common problems:**
- Changing a photographer's **URL** breaks old links to their page.
- The trash icon removes a photographer from the list. Nothing is saved until you click **Save**.
