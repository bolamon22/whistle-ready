Communications puts the game day tools in one place, with four tabs across the top.

**Where:** **Live → Communications**, or **Field Request** on a staff member's home page.

**The tabs:**
- **Field Request**: quick requests to staff, like balls, water or a trainer to a field. See [Send a field request](/tournaments/*/ops).
- **Broadcast**: post an announcement on the public page and, if you choose, alert the phones following those teams. Pick the **Audience** (**Everyone**, **Division**, **Team**, **Coaches** or **Staff**), write the message, optionally **Mark urgent**, and tap **Send broadcast**.
- **Incidents**: log medical, safety, facility or weather issues. See [Log an incident](/tournaments/*/incidents).
- **Contacts**: the staff directory with call and text buttons. See [Find and call staff](/tournaments/*/directory).

**Common problems:**
- **Broadcast** shows **Your role isn't currently allowed to broadcast** for roles the director hasn't allowed. The director can turn it on under **Setup → Tournament setup → Broadcast permissions**.
- **Alert followers' phones** is on by default when someone in the audience follows a team. Turn it off to post quietly. **Coaches** and **Staff** broadcasts never alert phones.
