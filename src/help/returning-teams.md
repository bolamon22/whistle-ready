Returning teams compares clubs from past tournaments with this one's registrations, and emails an invite to the clubs that haven't come back yet.

**Where:** People → Team registrations → **Returning teams**

**To invite clubs back:**
1. Under **Pull teams from which tournaments?**, tick one or more past events. A club that played several shows up once and gets one invite.
2. Review the list. **Status** shows **Registered** or **Not yet**. Filter with **All**, **Registered** and **Not Yet**.
3. Tick clubs, or click **Select all N unregistered**.
4. Open **Invite Email Template**, pick a letter (**Welcome back**, **First-time invite**, **Our full schedule**, **Last call**, **Short note**) and edit the **Subject** and **Body**. Variables like {{contactName}} and {{registerUrl}} fill in per club. Check the **Preview**.
5. Choose **Send now** or **Schedule**, then click **Send Invites (N)** or **Schedule (N)**.

**To keep your wording:** click **Save changes**, or **Save as new...** to keep it under a new name. **Reset to the original** brings back the built-in wording.

**To cancel a scheduled invite:** click **Cancel** next to it under **SCHEDULED INVITES**.

**Common problems:**
- Invites email clubs as soon as you click send.
- Clubs already registered are skipped even if ticked; the count says how many.
- Duplicate email addresses are only emailed once.
