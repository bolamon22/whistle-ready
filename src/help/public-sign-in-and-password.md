Club directors, coaches, parents and staff all sign in on the same page. Once you're signed in, you're taken to your own dashboard automatically.

**Where:** **Log in** at the top or bottom of your tournament organizer's website (on a phone, tap the person icon at the top), whistleready.app/login, or any **Sign in** button in the app or your emails.

**Sign in:**
1. Enter your email and password. Tap the eye icon to show the password while you type.
2. Tap **Sign In**. You'll land on the page for your account type (for example, your club portal or parent dashboard).

**First time signing in (club directors):**
1. Can't find the account setup email from your registration? On the sign-in page, tap **Email my portal link**.
2. Enter the email you used on your team registration and tap **Email my portal link**.
3. Open the email. **Open my club portal** lets you choose a password and takes you into your club portal. If you already have an account, the email has a **Sign in** button and a link to choose a new password instead.

The page says the same thing whether or not the email is on a registration, and it sends one email per address every 10 minutes.

**Forgot your password:**
1. On the sign-in page, tap **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email and tap **Email me a reset link**.
3. Open the email and tap the link. It works once and expires in 1 hour.
4. On **Choose a new password**, enter a new password (6+ characters), confirm it, and tap **Set new password**.
5. You'll see **Password updated — taking you to sign in…**. Sign in with the new password.

**Common problems:**
- "Invalid email or password." Check the email for typos, use the eye icon to check the password, or reset it.
- No reset email? Check spam. The email only goes out if that address has an account.
- No portal link email? Check spam, and make sure it's the address on your team registration. Wait 10 minutes before asking again.
- "This reset link is invalid or expired." Tap **Request a new one**.
- "Your team is linked. Sign in once more and you'll land in your club portal." This appears after claiming a team; just sign in again.
