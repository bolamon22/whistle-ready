**Divisions & teams** is where you see each division's teams, sort them into pools, and build games and brackets.

**Where:** **Setup → Divisions & teams**. Pick a division on the left. Each division has three tabs: **Teams & Pools**, **Games** and **Bracket**.

**To add, rename or delete a division:** click **+ Add Division** at the bottom of the list. Hover a division to see **Rename** and **Delete**. Deleting a division also removes its pools (you are asked to confirm).

**To create pools:** on **Teams & Pools**, type a name in **Add pool…** and click **+ Add**. Rename or delete a pool with its buttons. Deleting a pool clears its team assignments.

**To put teams in pools:**
1. Click **Assign Pools** to see pool columns.
2. Drag teams between pools, or click **Auto-assign teams** to spread them evenly.
3. Click **List view** to go back to the table. In the table you can also pick a pool from each team's **Pool** dropdown.

An amber "unassigned" count shows how many teams still need a pool.

**To swap two teams between pools:** in the list, click one team row, then a second, then click the **Swap** button that names both teams. **x Cancel** stops.

**To add a team by hand:** click **+ Add Team**. Only **Team Name** is required; **Club Name**, **Coach Name**, **Coach Phone** and **Coach Email** can come later. Click **Edit** on a team row to change its details or add a **Team Logo**.

**To move a team to another division:** click **Move** on its row, pick the **Destination Division**, and click **Move Team**.

**To add a note to the public schedule:** above the tabs, under **Notes on the public schedule**, click **Note for this division** or **Note for all divisions**, type it and click **Save**. On the public schedule page, each division shows its own note, then the note for all divisions, at the top. They show once **Teams & pools** is public. Click the pencil to change a note; save it empty to remove it.

**Common problems:**
- **Waiting list** teams are not in the draw. Clear the waitlist flag in **People → Team registrations** to place them.
- **Unconfirmed** teams were added without full details. Click **Edit**, fill them in and save to confirm.
- **Delete** on a team row also removes it from registrations (you are asked to confirm).
- If no divisions show, set them up in **Setup → Tournament setup → Divisions** (**Set up in Builder**).
- The **Divisions note** in **Setup → Tournament setup** is a different note: it shows under the division list on the event page, not on the schedule.
