When you send **Notify officials** with **Ask them to confirm their games** ticked, each official gets a **Confirm my games** button. This page shows who has confirmed, who hasn't answered, and which games people can't work.

**Where:** on the Assigner, click **Confirmed N/N** next to **Notify officials**. A "can't work" email also links here.

**At the top:**
- **Confirmed**: officials who have confirmed every game they have.
- **No answer yet**: officials with games they haven't answered. **Email the N** sends each of them a reminder with their games and the Confirm button.
- **Can't work**: games officials turned down, how many people, and how many are still open.
- **Games changed**: officials whose game moved (day, time or field) after they confirmed it. **Ask them again** emails just them.

**Each row** shows the official's answer: **Confirmed** and when, **No answer yet** and when they were emailed, **Confirmed 4 of 5**, **Game changed** with the new time, or **Can't work #205** with their reason and **Still open** or **Filled by** a name.

**Buttons on a row:**
- **Remind** or **Ask again** emails that one official.
- **Mark confirmed** is for someone who confirmed by phone or text. It confirms their games under your name.
- **Fill #205** opens the Assigner on that game's day.

**What a "can't work" does:** the game comes off that official's schedule right away, so the slot shows open on the Assigner (it reads **open · Brooks can't**) and nobody is paid for a game they didn't work. Whoever sent Notify officials gets an email and, with Whistle Ready alerts on, a phone alert. On the board, a **✓** after a name means that official confirmed the game.

**Common problems:**
- **Nobody has been asked to confirm yet.** Send **Notify officials** with **Ask them to confirm their games** ticked.
- Officials can't turn a game down within 48 hours of its day; they're told to call or text you.
- Only directors, admins and assigners see this page.
