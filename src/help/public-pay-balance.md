The pay link lets a club director pay a team registration balance online, without logging in.

**Where:** the pay button in a payment email from the organizer (it shows your balance, for example **Pay ... online**). The link is tied to your club's registration, so there is nothing to log in to.

1. Open the link. Under **Invoice** you see your club, your teams and divisions, **Invoiced**, **Paid to date** and **Balance due**.
2. Under **Payment**, choose **Bank transfer (ACH)**, **Card** or **PayPal / Venmo**. Bank transfer has no fee. Card and PayPal / Venmo add a processing fee, and the **Total due today** line shows the final amount.
3. Bank transfer: enter the **Account holder name** and **Email**, tap **Connect bank account**, log in to your bank through Stripe, then tap **Pay**.
4. Card: enter your **Card Details** and tap **Pay**.
5. PayPal / Venmo: finish in the PayPal window that opens.

You will see **Payment received — thank you!** when a card or PayPal payment goes through.

**Common problems:**
- **Bank transfer (ACH) initiated** means the payment is on its way. It usually clears in about 4 business days and is marked paid automatically.
- Opened the link again while your bank transfer is clearing? It says **Your bank transfer is on its way**, and there's nothing else to pay. If you still owe part of the balance, the transfer shows as **Bank transfer clearing** and **Balance due** is only what's left.
- **One more step — verify your bank account** means Stripe is sending a small deposit to your bank (1–2 business days). Follow the emailed instructions to finish.
- **Paid in full** or **No balance due** means there is nothing to pay. If that looks wrong, contact the organizer.
- **Payment link not found** means the link does not match a registration. Ask the organizer for a new link.
- The page also shows a Zelle option; put your club name in the memo.
