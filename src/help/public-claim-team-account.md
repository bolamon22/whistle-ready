After you register a team, you get an email with a link to set up your team account. That account is how you manage rosters and waivers, see your invoice and balance, and view the schedule.

**Where:** the account setup link in your registration email.

1. Open the link. You'll see **Set up your team account** with your club's name.
2. **Email** is filled in for you; it's the address used to register.
3. New to the app: enter **Your name**, then **Create a password** (at least 8 characters), and tap **Create account**.
4. Already have an account with that email: enter **Your existing password** and tap **Link this team to my account**.
5. You'll see **You're all set!** and be taken to your team dashboard. If you're asked to sign in again, do so; you'll land in your club portal.

The link is unique to your registration and can only be used once.

**Common problems:**
- **Already claimed** means the team is already linked to an account. Tap **Sign in**.
- **Link not valid** means the link is wrong or no longer works. Contact the tournament office.
- Forgot the password for your existing account? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page first.
