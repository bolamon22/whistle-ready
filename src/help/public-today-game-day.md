The Game day screen is the phone view of an event: your team's next game, its games with scores, and one-tap buttons for the player waiver, directions and the field map. No login needed.

**Where:** During an event (from two days before it starts through the last day), opening the event page or the organization's website on a phone goes straight here. You can also tap **Game day hub** on the event page. It is at /tournaments/<id>/today.

1. Under **Find your team**, type your club or team name and tap your team. The phone remembers it, so every later visit opens on your team.
2. The **YOUR TEAM** card shows the record and **UP NEXT**: opponent, day and time, field, and a countdown (for example "in 25 min", or **Live now**). Tap **Directions** or **Field map**.
3. Below it, every game for your team is listed by day, with **W** or **L** and the score once it is final.
4. Use the buttons: **Player waiver**, **Full schedule** (opens your team's games), **Standings**, **Directions**, **Field map** and **Rules**. Every button stays in the mobile view: the schedule and standings (every division's pools, games and brackets), the field maps, the rules and the waiver each open under a slim header. Tap **Game day** at the top left to come back, or **Full site** for the same page on the full site. **Directions** opens your maps app.
5. Tap **Change** to pick a different team.
6. Staff, referees, coaches and club directors: tap **Log in** at the top.
7. Tap **Full site** for everything else on the event page. On the full site, the **Mobile view** button at the bottom left of a phone screen brings you back here.
8. Turn off **Always open the schedule first** if you'd rather your phone open the full site. Tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**, to keep this screen one tap away.

**Common problems:**
- **Looking for a different event?** Before you pick a team, the top shows **Looking for the next one?** with the organization's next event. Tap it.
- **No team matches.** Try your club's name. Teams appear once the schedule is published.
- **The schedule hasn't been posted yet.** means games are not published. Check back later.

**For directors:** a director signed in on this screen sees **Staff: phones are sent here** with **Turn off**, which stops phones going here for this event (and **Turn on** to bring it back). It can take up to a minute to take effect.
