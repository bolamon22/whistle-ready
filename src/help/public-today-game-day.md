The Today page is a quick game-day screen: the next game coming up, the latest announcement, and one-tap buttons for directions, field maps and more.

**Where:** **Game day hub** on the event page (in the **Event info** menu or under **For players & parents**), or the **Game day** link in your registration email. It is at /tournaments/<id>/today.

1. The top card shows the **NEXT GAME**: teams, day and time, field and division, plus a countdown (for example "in 25 min", or **Live now**). The game after it is listed below.
2. Tap **View full schedule** for every game, standings and brackets.
3. The latest organizer announcement shows under the game card.
4. Use the buttons: **My Teams**, **Directions**, **Field maps**, **Rules**, **Merchandise** and **Parking**.
5. Tap **View full event page** for everything else about the event.

**Common problems:**
- **The schedule hasn't been posted yet.** means games are not published. Check back later.
- **No upcoming games — the tournament may be complete.** means every game has a score.
- To track one team, follow it on the schedule page and use **My Teams** there.
