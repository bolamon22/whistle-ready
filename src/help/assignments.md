The Assignments page is a read-and-tidy view of who is on each game. Use it to check coverage or see one person's whole schedule.

**Where:** **Live → Assignments**.

**To check coverage by game:**
1. Click **By game**.
2. Pick a day with the date buttons (click it again to show all days) and narrow with **All divisions**.
3. Each game shows its filled slots, for example 2/3. Green means full, amber means partly filled, red means nobody. The top of the page shows how many slots are filled overall.

**To see each person's schedule:** click **By staff**. Each person lists their games in time order with their role.

**To remove an assignment:** click the X next to the name. To add or swap people, use **Setup → Assigner**.

**Common problems:**
- The X removes the assignment right away with no confirmation.
- **No staff assigned** means the game has no one yet.
