The booking board is for the event's housing company: one private page with every upcoming event's clubs, where you log the hotels each club books.

**Where:** the private link the organizer sends you. There's no account or password, so keep the link to yourself. The organizer can replace the link, which turns the old one off.

1. The counts at the top show how many clubs need hotels, are in progress or are booked, and the total room nights.
2. Tap **All clubs** or a status to show just those clubs. Clubs that still need hotels are listed first.
3. Set each club's **Status**: **Needs hotels**, **In progress**, **Booked** or **Local — not needed**.
4. Under **Hotels**, type the hotel, the **rooms** and the **nights**. Tap **Add hotel** when a club splits across several hotels; the **×** removes one.
5. Put anything else under **Notes**.

Everything saves as you go (**Saved** shows for a moment), and the organizer sees it in Whistle Ready right away.

**Block hotels:** each event's block hotels are listed with it. Tap **Mark sold out** when one fills up, and the event's hotel page shows it as sold out right away.

**Common problems:**
- "That didn't save. Check your connection and try again." Your change didn't reach the organizer: check your connection and make it again.
- An event isn't on the board: it shows once clubs have registered for it or it has block hotels.
