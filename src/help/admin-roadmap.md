The **Feature Roadmap** is an internal board for tracking feature requests and fixes.

**Where:** top bar → **Roadmap** (admins only).

**To add an item:**
1. Under **Add Feature Request**, enter a **Title** (required), and optionally a description and an estimate (for example 1 hr).
2. Click **+ Add Item**.

**To work an item:**
- Set its status to **To Do**, **In Progress** or **Done**.
- Click **Notes & Subtasks** to add **Notes** (they save as you type) and **Subtasks**.
- Click **Edit** to change it, then **Save**.
- Click **Delete** to remove it.

**To find items:** use **Search tasks…**, filter by status, and sort by **Newest first**, **Oldest first** or **By status**.

**Common problems:**
- Delete removes the item and all its subtasks after a confirmation.
