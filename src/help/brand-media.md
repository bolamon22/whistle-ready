The **Brand & media library** keeps your logos, documents, maps and promo graphics in one place so you can reuse them on your event pages. Image and file pickers across the app can pull from here.

**Where:** Home → **Admin** → **Brand & media** (admins), or the **Brand & media** button on the **Website** page.

**To upload files:**
1. Pick a category in **Add to**: **Logos**, **Documents**, **Maps** or **Promo**.
2. Click **Upload files** and choose one or more images or PDFs.

**To find a file:** click **All** or a category to filter, or type in **Search…**.

**To manage a file:**
- Click the name to rename it. It saves when you click away.
- Change its category from the menu under it.
- Use **Copy link** to copy its web address, **Open** to view it, or **Delete** to remove it.

**Common problems:**
- Large photos are shrunk automatically before upload.
- Delete asks you to confirm and cannot be undone.
