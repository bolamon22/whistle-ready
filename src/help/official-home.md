When a ref, scorekeeper, trainer or field ops worker logs in, they land on their own home page with their events, their next game and the game day tools.

**Where:** it opens when you log in.

**To sign up for an event:**
1. Under **My events**, tap **Sign up to work** on the event.
2. Once you're on it, it shows **Working** and how many games you're assigned. Tap **Can't work it** to take yourself off (only before you have games).

**To see your games:**
- **Up next** shows your next game with time, field and teams.
- Tap **My games** for your full list, or **All games** and pick an event to see every game.

**To open the scorer:** on a game where you're the scorekeeper, tap **Open scorer**. See [Keep score live during a game](/tournaments/*/scoring).

**To clock in (field ops and athletic trainers):** under **Time clock**, tap **Clock In** when you start and **Clock Out** when you finish.

**Game day tools:** buttons for **Field Request**, **Live scoring**, **Post scores**, **Staff contacts**, **Setup checklist** and **Incidents**. You only see the tools for your job.

**My staff ID** opens your printable badge. See [Print your staff ID or add it to Apple Wallet](/dashboard/staff/id-card).

**Common problems:**
- **Your login isn't linked to a staff record yet.** Contact your coordinator.
- **Check in** marks the game on your phone only; it doesn't notify anyone.
- **No upcoming assignments.** You haven't been put on a game yet.
