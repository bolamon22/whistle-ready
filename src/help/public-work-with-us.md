We hire referees, scorekeepers, athletic trainers and event staff for our tournaments. Send an application and we'll be in touch about open positions.

**Where:** **Work With Us** in the website menu.

1. Under **About you**, fill in **Full name**, **Email** and **Mobile phone**.
2. Under **Which events?**, select the upcoming events you'd like to work.
3. Under **Positions**, select every role you're interested in.
4. If you picked a referee or official role, fill in **Officiating details**: **I officiate** (**Boys**, **Girls** or **Both**) and your **Certification level**.
5. Under **Experience & availability**, add your relevant experience, which days you can work, and any certifications (CPR, trainer certs and so on).
6. Add anything else in **Anything else?**, then tick the age confirmation box.
7. Tap **Submit application**. You'll see a confirmation (by default, **Application received!**).

This form is an application, not an account. If you're brought on, you'll get a staff signup or invite link (see "Join the staff from a signup or invite link").

**Common problems:**
- "Please choose at least one event", "Please choose at least one position" or "Please confirm the age requirement" means something required is missing.
- No **Work With Us** in the menu means staff applications are closed right now.
