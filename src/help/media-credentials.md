Media credentials is where photographers and creators apply to shoot your event. You review each one and credential the ones you want on the field.

**Where:** People → Media credentials

**To review an application:**
1. Click a row to see their details. Status shows **Needs review**, **Credentialed** or **Declined**.
2. Click **Open portfolio** to see their work.
3. Click **Credential & send pass** and confirm, or **Decline** and confirm.
4. **Undo** puts it back to **Needs review**. **Delete** removes it for good.

**To resend a credential:** click **Copy credential link** on an approved applicant and send it yourself.

**To invite someone to shoot:**
1. Open **Invite someone to shoot**.
2. Pick a letter (for example **Come shoot with us**, **Back again**, **Short note**, **Join for the season**).
3. Under **Who gets it**, paste one or more emails and click **Add**.
4. Edit the **Subject** and **Body** and check the preview. Tick **Remember this wording** to keep it.
5. Click **Send** and confirm. Or click **Copy letter** to send it from your own inbox.

**Other tools:** **Open form** opens the application form. **Export CSV** downloads the list.

**Common problems:**
- **Credential & send pass** emails them their credential page with field rules and check-in details.
- **Decline** sends no email.
- People already invited for this event are skipped and listed under **Already invited**.
