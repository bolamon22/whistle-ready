**Tasks** is your to-do list for each event: field permits, rentals, staff, club emails and grant paperwork. Each task has a due date, and the red number on **Tasks** is how many are overdue.

**Where:** **Tasks** in the top bar shows every event. A tournament's own list is the **Tasks** tab in its menu. Both the **Tournaments** page and a tournament's **Dashboard** have a **Tasks** box with what's up next.

**To start an event's list from the template:**
1. On the event's **Tasks** tab, click **Choose items** (or **Add from template** once it has tasks). On the all-events page, click the event's name under **Start from the checklist template**.
2. Each item's due date counts back from the first game day. Items already due before today start unticked; tick any that still need doing.
3. Click **Add** at the bottom (it shows how many, like **Add 12 tasks**). Items the event already has are skipped.

To see the whole template first, click **Checklist template** and pick an event under **Preview due dates for**.

**To add your own task:** type it in the box at the top of the list, pick a category and a due date, then click **Add**. On the all-events page, also pick the event, or **General** for a task that isn't tied to one.

**To work a task:**
- Click the circle to check it off. Click it again to reopen it.
- Click the task to open its details: change the title, **Due** date or **Category**, add **Steps** and **Notes**, or **Delete** it.
- The button on a task (like **Email clubs** or **Scheduler**) opens the page where it gets done.

**Tracked tasks** show what Whistle Ready can see, like "4 clubs unpaid". Four check themselves off: **Publish the schedule** (when you publish), **Payment reminder to unpaid clubs** (when every club has paid), **Collect unpaid balances** (when every balance is paid) and **Officials assigned to every game** (when every game has its refs). If you reopen one, it stays open.

**Setup checklist:** each upcoming event also shows its shared setup checklist as one task, due the day before. It sits under the list on the event's **Tasks** tab, and staff check it off from **Setup → Checklist**.

**To sort the list:** use **Group by** (**Due date**, **Category** or **Event**). On the all-events page, the buttons along the top show one event or **General**. **Done** starts folded (and **Later**, on the all-events page); click a heading to open it.

**Common problems:**
- Only directors and admins have **Tasks**. Staff still open the setup checklist the way they always have.
- A task with no event appears under **General**, not on any tournament's tab.
