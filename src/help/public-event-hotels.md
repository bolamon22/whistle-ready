The event's hotel page lists the hotels holding a room block for the event, with the event rate and a button to book each one.

**Where:** **Book hotels** on the event page, or the hotel link in your club's emails.

1. Each hotel shows its **Event rate** per night. Tap **See rates & book** (or **Book**) to see the rates and book through the event's housing partner.
2. A hotel marked **Sold out** has no rooms left in its block. Pick another one.
3. To narrow the list, use **Sort** (**Recommended**, **Closest** or **Lowest price**) and the chain, distance and price filters. **View map** shows where they are; **Clear filters** starts over.
4. **Good to know** has the event's notes about hotels and travel.
5. Need something else? **See more hotels near the fields** opens a wider search.

**Common problems:**
- The event rate lasts only while the room block does. Book early.
- No **See rates & book** on a hotel: its block is sold out, or it has no booking link yet. Pick another, or try **See more hotels near the fields**.
