The scorekeeper screen can run a countdown for each penalty and show the tournament rules without leaving the game.

**Where:** the scorekeeper screen for your game.

**To start a penalty:**
1. Tap **+ Penalty** under the team.
2. Enter the **Player #** (optional).
3. Under **Duration**, tap 0:30, 1:00, 2:00, 3:00 or 5:00, or tap **Custom time…** and set **Min** and **Sec**.
4. Tap **Start Penalty**. Tick **Keep open to stack another penalty** to add more in a row, then tap **Done**.

**While a penalty runs:** use the pause button to hold it, **−15s** and **+15s** to adjust, the pencil to set an exact time, and the X to remove it. A penalty drops off when it reaches zero. Penalty timers only count down while the game clock is running.

**To read the rules:** tap **Rules** at the bottom, then **Back to scoring**.

**Settings (gear button at the top):**
- **Ask for player # when a goal is scored**
- **No ties — warn at final if the score is tied**
- **Official game time kept on field by refs**
- **Game format**: **Halves**, **Quarters**, **Periods** or **Running clock (no periods)**
- **Tournament rules**: paste the rules and tap **Save rules**.

Tap **Done** to close.

**Common problems:**
- Penalties and goal-scorer numbers are kept on this phone only and are lost if the page reloads.
- **Ask for player #** and the default penalty and period times are remembered on this phone only, even though they say tournament default. The other settings and the rules are saved for the whole tournament.
